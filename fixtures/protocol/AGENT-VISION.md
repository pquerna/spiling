# Original protocol interoperability fixtures vision

Provide a small deterministic cross-language corpus for packed-data interoperability: diagnostic SPLT, independent algebraic multi-face SPLM/outer-hole SPLS, and actual native box/through-hole SPLM/SPLS including complete loops in multiple chunks. The native family connects source hashes and numerical geometry to independently decoded bytes; neither family proves responsive engine transport, GPU presentation or manufacturing correctness. Real process and platform acceptance remains a separate evidence gate.
