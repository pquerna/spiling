# Original protocol interoperability fixtures vision

Provide a small deterministic cross-language corpus for packed-data interoperability: diagnostic SPLT, independent algebraic multi-face SPLM/outer-hole SPLS, actual native box/through-hole SPLM/SPLS including complete loops in multiple chunks, and independently decoded actual-program SPLR motion. Native fixtures connect source/program hashes and numerical values to independently decoded bytes; they do not prove responsive engine transport, GPU presentation or manufacturing correctness. Real process and platform acceptance remains a separate evidence gate.
