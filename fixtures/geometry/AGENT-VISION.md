# Original native geometry corpus vision

Make unit, topology, source-face mapping, carrier-error, trim and native-section claims reproducible. Analytic valid fixtures and one-property adversaries bound the admitted profile. A stress fixture is a transfer workload, not production geometry or a printable-product claim.

Frozen original bytes retain their historical builder identity, exact circles, physical units and adversarial record mutations. Verify their manifests independently of current geometry inspection. No legacy builder or synthetic success matrix is required to establish current support; meaningful real CAD and unique consumer invariants remain the acceptance inputs.
