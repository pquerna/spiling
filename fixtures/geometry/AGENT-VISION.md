# Original native geometry corpus vision

Make unit, topology, source-face mapping, carrier-error, trim and native-section claims reproducible. Analytic valid fixtures and one-property adversaries bound the admitted profile. A stress fixture is a transfer workload, not production geometry or a printable-product claim.

The recipe uses public generic STEP geometry builders to retain exact circles, and exact analytic source-construction normalization of planar/circular extrusion carriers before serialization. Inch coordinates and declared uncertainty are genuinely scaled through a controlled fixed-ID conversion-unit template. Adversaries mutate the parsed original record structure rather than repairing input or borrowing third-party CAD.
