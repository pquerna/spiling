# Native wall certification vision

Give native consumers a narrow source-local rectangular-face certificate without exposing SolidCore or modifying source geometry. Exact trim correspondence, outward direction and physical extent bind finite mechanical patches; a whole-part consumer separately proves full-height coverage. An exterior-wall certificate additionally proves complete source height and global support for manufacturing finishes; trim coverage alone never grants that authority.

The current envelope is a whole full-height vertical planar rectangle. More general trims, cylinders, partial-height regions and crease-spanning treatments need separate native certificates and acceptance; successful face certification alone does not establish a valid manufactured solid or printer support.
