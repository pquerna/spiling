# Native wall certification rules

- Certify a source face through the admitted face-to-native-topology mapping, never carrier proximity, display vertices or section-loop ordinals. Borrow immutable kernel definitions; export only Spiling-owned physical certificate data.
- Both rectangular-face and exterior-wall certificates require one connected rectangular outer trim, distinct corners and local-Z vertical edges. Rectangular-face coverage encloses each complete native UV-on-carrier boundary, its directional derivative and endpoints; neither samples nor carrier proximity prove the trim. The exterior-wall path retains its original bounded affine spatial correspondence checks; native curve enums and natural intervals alone are not bounded-edge authority.
- A rectangular-face receipt grants only selected-trim coverage; its consumer must compare the retained height with independently certified whole-source coverage. The separate exterior-wall certificate additionally requires full analytic source height and the oriented global supporting material side, refusing additional coplanar faces, recesses and partial-height faces. Both refuse trim holes and sloped/cylindrical carriers.
- Admission residual is 1e-7 mm; angular boundary is 1e-10. Validate finite coefficients/spans and poll cancellation at native face/edge/witness boundaries. These bounds do not enlarge manufacturing offset or surface approximation budgets.
- Whole-source support is an exclusion-only envelope of retained analytic plane/cylinder spatial boundaries, not a raw directional-bounds receipt or UV embedding proof. Selected wall straightness still borrows its original correspondence owner; reject witnessed recesses and coplanar ownership before that preparation. Retained proof and scratch share the fixed allocation cap.
- The certificate establishes only the selected face. Manufacturing separately proves extrusion eligibility, pose, source/derived ownership, protected holes, overlap and printer-envelope constraints. Do not claim physical printability or tactile quality.
- Exercise the public geometry facade with original and independently exported native sources, rejected trims/carriers, unknown identity and cancellation; recorded corpus evidence belongs in [geometry evidence](../../../../../docs/quality/geometry-evidence.md).
