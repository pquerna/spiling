# Native wall certification rules

- Certify a source face through the admitted face-to-native-topology mapping, never carrier proximity, display vertices or section-loop ordinals. Borrow immutable kernel definitions; export only Spiling-owned physical certificate data.
- Require one connected four-line rectangular outer trim, distinct corners, local-Z vertical edges, full analytic source height and the oriented global supporting material side. Additional coplanar faces, trim holes, recesses, sloped/cylindrical or partial-height carriers refuse explicitly.
- Admission residual is 1e-7 mm; angular boundary is 1e-10. Validate finite coefficients/spans and poll cancellation at native face/edge/witness boundaries. These bounds do not enlarge manufacturing offset or surface approximation budgets.
- The certificate establishes only the selected face. Manufacturing separately proves extrusion eligibility, pose, source/derived ownership, protected holes, overlap and printer-envelope constraints. Do not claim physical printability or tactile quality.
- Exercise the public geometry facade with original and independently exported native sources, rejected trims/carriers, unknown identity and cancellation; recorded corpus evidence belongs in [geometry evidence](../../../../../docs/quality/geometry-evidence.md).
