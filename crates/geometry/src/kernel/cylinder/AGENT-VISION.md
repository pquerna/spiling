# Full-cylinder face certificate vision

Provide a source-backed native proof usable by curved manufacturing consumers without leaking the kernel or replacing source attribution. The first consumer realizes constant-Z grip ribs; the underlying face-associated analytic trim references remain general for every admitted line/circle face.

Current certification covers a complete outward circular cylinder spanning native source Z. Its physical rim/seam incidence proof admits arbitrary valid subdivisions and chart placement rather than a frozen exporter topology. Partial/split/recessed/inward/sloped or ambiguous face uses are explicit unsupported cases, not repaired/merged faces. Broader admission needs its own exact coverage proof; it must not require replacing overlay ownership or downstream material-domain planning. Physical grip and printer qualification remain separate gates.
