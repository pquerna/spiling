# Private kernel ownership vision

Keep Monstertruck ownership narrow and inspectable. Analytical carrier and boundary information supports independent mesh-deviation audits and safe native section queries without a second geometry authority. Periodic edge representation must preserve source face identity and carrier orientation through native extraction and modeling transforms; qualification includes independent exporter and malformed-topology evidence, not a filename-dependent admission lane.

Provide source-backed face-only rectangular wall certification through the public geometry facade, retaining positional native topology and oriented supporting-plane authority. A certificate gives downstream phase placement and exterior direction without kernel exports, source mutation or mesh/section inference. More general trims, holes, recess carriers, co-carrier ambiguity, sloped and partial-height walls stay outside this certificate's support envelope.

Provide source-backed full-cylinder certification separately from rectangular walls. Preserve general face/wire/coedge analytic associations for every admitted line/circle face, so downstream exact queries need neither a second native representation nor geometric attribution guesses. Physical rim cycles and paired identical seam incidences, not a hard-coded chart layout, define full periodic coverage; native carrier orientation excludes bores, and analytic whole-source extrema exclude short/recessed trims. Manufacturing independently proves section exterior ownership, invariant extrusion, pose and process bounds.

The periodic support gate includes native horizontal, oblique and placed first-layer material sections of unchanged independent cylinder and through-hole sources. Shared-edge subdivision and chart-corner attachment are topology representation operations on original transformed/oriented carriers, not source healing or a display-mesh section authority. A passing import or mesh alone does not qualify the native boolean path.
