# Declared STEP admission rules

[ADR005](../../../../docs/adr/ADR005-native-first-development-geometry.md) adopts the active native-first DEVELOPMENT material authority. The source-relative correspondence rules below govern inspection and original finish inputs, not reconstruction of a second material solid. Preserve source identity/provenance and declared associations; derived/native trims must not silently change the authoritative BREP or masquerade as original STEP declarations. Rational surface admission may apply one exactly reversible binary homogeneous gauge to fit the native canonical weight envelope; retain every relative weight and weighted coordinate, otherwise refuse. In-envelope inputs keep their existing representation.

Parse UTF-8 once through maintained step-p21 after source/entity lexical caps; require one DATA section and declared AP203/AP214 profile. Qualified schema OIDs must match the named ISO/AP family and contain positive edition/conformance arcs; do not assume a fixed three-arc suffix. Corresponding pcurve representation contexts are metadata, not additional carrier support. Resolve referenced length/radian units and uncertainty in that AST. Refuse assemblies, unsupported reachable geometry, duplicate/missing canonical face uses and reachable conversion loss. Check remaining native-face capacity before conversion, then normalize through public modeling transforms and require valid closed solid plus certified bounds.

`mod.rs` owns native admission and positional source/native face assertions; `ast.rs` owns bounded lexical/AST traversal; `units.rs` resolves only solid-referenced representation contexts. Keep declared mm/metre/inch length units, radian angles and uncertainty provenance. Face family/optional analytic metadata is inspection data, never operation permission. Admit bounded native plane/cylinder/cone/sphere/regular-ring torus, polynomial/rational splines and exact sweeps through kernel-reported conversion maps; reachable unimplemented wrappers still identify their source entity in the refusal.

Retain shared curve source/native intervals and exact conversion maps before rationalization; immutable public source-unit provenance owns the common mm scale, and source topology owns original selectors, wrapper genealogy and use orientations. Bind the retained maps' restricted decoded-source ranges to that lineage and their native IDs/senses to the final normalized coedges; incidental BREP convenience ranges never supply source trim authority. Source UV and rational native UV are not interchangeable: never certify conversion by evaluating both at the same sampled parameters. Keep exact native chart curves and their full selected branches without copied carrier surfaces or BREP per coedge; no separate periodic-lift metadata substitutes for the expression. Bound retained wires/coedges at `2 * MAX_STEP_ENTITIES`, poll cancellation, and bind each oriented source occurrence to native edge/vertex identity and endpoints. Wrapper removal requires whole-curve analytic carrier membership; unsupported implicit correspondence refuses explicitly.

An original unbounded UV LINE's finite active branch may reuse the exact whole natural-row/all-knot coefficient theorem when its complete affine declaration and oriented endpoint coefficients agree, without asserting global C1 or manufacturing a forward-error receipt. Preserve nonzero source/chart uncertainty and the original denominator/support obligations. A periodic covering translation is an explicit outer operator around the already admitted original LINE, raw map, active interval and reflection anchors; literal bounded declarations cannot move. Every proposed lift still needs the complete inverse tube, forward position/relative-tangent proof and original shared-vertex joint. A spherical pole endpoint is only a chart proposal until whole incident source correspondence and the actual native incoming/outgoing pole completion qualify its complete connector image; interior pole crossings remain refusals.

Pole-meridian graphs have distinct canonical chart authority, never ordinary inverse authority. Prove a positive whole original projected radius in the analytic meridian plane, signed phase monotonicity and a latitude tube over the complete original gauge; bound transverse/radial displacement in the original affine frame and retain it as physical error. Whole original/native position and relative tangents remain mandatory. Publish no graph certificate until both actual source-vertex joints have Native whole-image proofs, with actual spherical-pole classification at every designated pole and the graph physical error reserved inside the unchanged joint budget. Preserve graph kind in coedge metadata, keep declared-source authority false, and exclude graphs from regular-coordinate shortcuts.

Periodic wire search preserves literal constant-anchor priority. Without such an anchor, a pending meridian with complete coefficient and forward/tangent proof may anchor at its actual oriented pole endpoint, using the proved native/UV endpoint pairing through all original reflections. This changes search order only: the whole ordinary boundary chain transports its original phase, including wide sectors, before the final original pole joint is qualified. Never choose a nearest longitude to shorten material, change an original arc, or replace final ordinary half-period, pole-image, source-vertex or winding gates.

Preserve the bounded native extraction cause in rejection diagnostics. Admission failures identify stage, original source face/edge or native slot, periodic candidate shift and cumulative work. Retain only fixed-size last/largest periodic interval-fallback receipts across admission, including successful fallbacks and their preceding harmonic numerical declines; a later entry-budget failure must not lose its actual prior work consumer. Charge receipt coexistence to proof scratch, format only on failure and never log every declined candidate. A kernel representation failure is not independent proof that the source solid is invalid; do not hide it with healing, source rewriting or guessed face remapping.

Selected-master checks retain at most one import-local whole-original-range forward position/relative-tangent receipt per stable original continuation owner. Every native child still resolves its actual selected association/domain; only exact finite operand, active-map, frame and UV-margin binding may reuse the completed proof. Charge receipt capacity beside original owners and drop it before publication. Exact coefficient projections, inverse tubes and another face's association are not those receipts. Collect correlated sweep coefficients before interval separation for position and derivative enclosures; retain native smooth-span and denominator qualifications. Explicit native harmonic comparisons must enclose both original expressions, complete affine phase/domain transport, numerical evaluation error and relative tangent agreement against a positive source-speed bound; unsupported expressions retain the bounded interval proof. Native parameter-curve operands already contain the public converter's composed original input chart.

A selected master's nominal proof may supply an initial child base only after that child's own declared face association is established. The parent's native/UV parameter law is identity. A child's exact-unit affine law may compose with finite identity-framed chart trim reversals only when error-free interval coefficient arithmetic proves literal `+t+0`, including both affine endpoint anchors, every intermediate trim domain and the complete oriented endpoint image. Non-point coefficient bounds, a residual offset, nonidentity spatial frames or unsupported composition are binding nonproof, never periodic aliases. Source restrictions remain forward and exactly identity-framed; the parent's completed domain and coefficient tube must cover the child. Bind all remaining curve, surface and chart operands to the same stable unmapped root owner/frame, using a finite import-only key distinct from normalized-publication binding. Recompute the child map/inventory; keep inverse, quality refinement and final full-tube tangents mandatory. A hinted sufficient nonproof restarts the original child-base path once; errors, cancellation and work exhaustion remain terminal.

Harmonic numerical reserves retain independent spatial-evaluation and phase-evaluation terms with the same operation-count bound. Phase error is amplified only by the outward sum of absolute trigonometric coefficients after the actual spatial map, never by world translation. Affine UV evaluation retains separate constant/slope majorants `B0+B1*|t|`: reflection adds only `B1*(|a|+|b|)` to the constant, spatial translation affects only that constant, and the complete target range scales only `B1`. Relative-tangent reserves also retain the phase-slope evaluation error when the target interval is small. Retain the spatial reserve even for equal or cancelling coefficients.

Retained implicit UV completion derives one same-branch base precision from the original inverse metric, coefficient uncertainty and admitted point diameter, without enlarging the final physical allowance. A completed refined-base whole-position receipt plus the expanded tube's complete first-jet displacement may prove final position only for the identical borrowed branch, curve/chart, active affine map and frame scale. Normalized publication may reuse a completed whole-forward source receipt only after a bounded finite native-expression binding proves its actual operands, owner, ranges, map, tube and frame unchanged; otherwise recompute in millimetres. The seal is import-local and never persistent authority. Reprove relative tangents on the complete actual final tube; neither a raw scalar residual nor inverse authority may replace the coupled forward receipt.

Physical position may factor through the same original owner's function-bound selected image: certify its whole 3D image discrepancy `delta` to the actual current image, then require original-root-to-selected-image position within the outward lower bound of `goal - delta`. A completed whole-image bound may restrict to the identical child function/owner/frame only when its achieved error already fits that remaining allowance; otherwise freshly prove the finer bound. Check the outward sum against the unchanged goal. Borrow the already-owned native image; neither its nominal conic window nor a coarse achieved error supplies new accuracy. Use only the fixed forward map, never the position-only API's reversed alternative. This triangle proof grants no UV/surface identity: current source binding, inverse, complete expanded-tube tangents and ordinary proof fallback remain mandatory.
At a new selected-plane owner, one conditional quality proposal uses complete original-carrier inverse/point/coefficient metrics and certified image discrepancies. Its hypothetical residual is not authority: an independent whole proof must achieve `A <= q <= R` before reuse. Half the remaining allowance is search headroom, not a bound on the actual periodic image. Attempt it before the ordinary proof to avoid duplicate coarse/strong work; ordinary nonproof takes that original path and errors propagate. Periodic completion borrows the same master/prepared original owner; never drop that authority or copy geometry.

Within one correspondence proof, a qualified smooth parent's complete residual-Hessian bound and, when required, relative-tangent proof may finish contained children using carried endpoint residuals, each evaluated once on the same actual tube. Endpoint target residuals request native position bounds on that full expanded UV tube; they grant no derivative or smoothness authority. A range-bound parent source jet minted from complete C1 qualification may also restrict SOURCE C1 to contained children; requalify each child's actual chart and surface box, and obtain the tighter native source jet on ordinary inherited-bound nonproof. With a complete coupled position receipt, an unchanged owner's single prepared piece may instead supply first derivatives for an accept-only full-tube relative-tangent proof. Preserve active ranges, scale and projection-joint barriers; mapped owners and broad first-jet nonproof use the fresh source query. Tangent-only jets supply no position, C1 or Taylor authority. Unqualified piecewise Hessians, changed operands/tubes and failed inherited bounds grant no authority. Charge fixed inherited-jet/remainder storage to the existing scratch cap.

A complete parent C1/full-residual-Hessian receipt may test one ordinary dyadic split before requesting another source jet: both outward child bounds must meet the unchanged goal. Coedges retain the parent's complete tangent proof; the private fixed-forward position proof grants no current UV authority. Retain normal stack/depth/node/work admission for the children. On nonproof, use the ordinary fresh-jet route and reuse the single evaluated midpoint only within that immutable proof. Neither a scalar position bound nor tangent-only receipt supplies this Taylor authority.

Analytic inverse-phase tubes may use a candidate reference only after validated sine/cosine rotates the complete XY enclosure into a strictly positive-X half-plane. Bound the angular residual outwardly by `|Y/X|`; otherwise retain native corner isolation. This is a whole-expression enclosure, never a sampled phase or permission to change an original interval, branch or forward-correspondence obligation.

The authoritative public STEP converter may represent a valid closed edge by two shared exact native subedges, including a closed rational spline without a declared period. Its regular domain is `[U_degree, U_control_count]`, not the exterior knot support. Every oriented use must retain the same subdivision, original source intervals, face count/order, carriers and face direction. Paired seam uses may explain repeated boundary vertices; arbitrary touching loops, seam spikes, open boundaries, collapsed/nonclosing self-loops and contradictory carriers remain refusals. Do not implement this representation with an adapter-side healer or second extraction path.

Import boxes and rational-denominator admission share a cumulative bounded interval pass over complete native curve/surface domains, seeded at original source knots before requesting strict jets. Mixed or all-negative weights are not automatic failures; subdivide until the actual denominator excludes zero, or report bounded conditioning failure. Bare sweep profiles' convenience ranges are not STEP trim authority: use the complete retained original UV boundary to bound the chart, then enclose the whole surface image; exact extrusions use the native ruling-boundary theorem. Display/section/normal regularity remains operation-specific; no finite sample or display mesh proves import bounds or chart coverage.
