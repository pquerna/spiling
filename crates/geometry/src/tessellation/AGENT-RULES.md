# Strict native display conversion rules

Mesh admitted faces through the pinned public trim-aware compressed-shell mesher, preserving every retained original per-use UV curve, source-face order and orientation; never discard those charts for fresh plain-shell reprojection. Borrow UV curves and carriers, precharge all temporary snapshot/view metadata, and release carrier guards before audit preparation. Reject absent/empty/non-triangle faces. Initial tolerance is one quarter of the 0.025 mm native display budget, triangles and two search trials. At most eight finer shape-error candidates share unchanged work, byte and mesh caps. Regular UV triangles use whole-domain outward native jets and interpolation remainders; the same barycentric transport pairs every triangle point with the complete source UV-triangle image and conversely. Trim and quantization remain independent gates. No redundant infinite-analytic-carrier guard, mesher tolerance or vertex-only curved check is a certificate.

`FaceInfo.geometry` is descriptive; native chart/trim evidence remains mandatory, including representation-equivalent splines and sweeps. Cached orientation is relative to its regular canonical carrier, not the kernel topology flag. Derive analytic normals from that carrier/sign, or use retained native orientation for generic carriers, and validate against native normals plus topology orientation. Inward hole normals remain inward. Reject malformed/nonfinite attributes/indices, nonunit normals, degenerate triangles, reversed winding and unresolved/singular charts rather than repairing them.

Before packing, prove UV triangle-chain orientation and interior-edge cancellation, simple boundary circuits, complete ordered native coedge interval correspondence, uniform material winding and one-to-one coverage of every native/mesh trim loop. Interval UV curve jets, knot partitions, shared native joint uncertainty and whole-coedge source-to-rounded-chart margins bound complete trim displacement; expand the surface derivative query over the complete source tube, not just nominal coefficients. Nearest-parameter searches only seed correspondence. `mesh-trimmed-mm-0.05-v2` records carrier and trim errors independently, with their sum ≤0.025 mm. Share one cumulative work allowance across faces, intervals and triangles. Remap only the current face into contracts' identity-free chunk builder; identical position/normal values may share a vertex. Float32 displacement is separately ≤0.025 mm and carrier-plus-trim-plus-quantization ≤0.05 mm. Enforce narrowed budgets; discard partial output on cancellation/error. Never mint session/artifact identities.

Source-unbound tensor seams may use `display_trim`'s ephemeral native covering rectangle only for one complete original boundary wire. Scope eligibility by an absent opposed pair of original source seam occurrences, not by requiring every absent chart on that wire to be a seam: ordinary rim uses can also lack derived charts. Every retained UV expression stays borrowed unchanged, and every display branch must prove complete homogeneous natural-row/knot equivalence to its unchanged native 3D edge. Complete ordered closure and all four natural sides precede the same native joint, triangle-chain, loop-bijection, whole-triangle error and normal audits. Additional wires/holes or unresolved row/interval ambiguity refuse this narrow lane; none are omitted. Display candidates never modify `Definition`, restore original PCURVE bindings, or satisfy an operation's `PreparedFaceTrimDomain` requirement.

`parametric::DisplayTriangleProof` borrows one immutable face and its mesh UV slice for one audit. A charged inventory binds triangle ordinals to exact UV-box keys and allocates dense proof slots only after discovering the actual unique count; discovery/key workspace is dropped before audit/output growth. Query ordinal, box key and UV-index bits must match. A primary slot may also retain only a completed auxiliary smooth/rank Hessian and its exact derived-box key; matching reuse still requires actual auxiliary point enclosures, fresh physical-vertex residuals and all original-box transport. Never retain triangle outputs. All actual retained/transient capacities, including fixed scratch and inline optional receipts, are precharged; borrowed UV storage remains charged by its mesh owner. Release the audit owner at exit.

Trim-joint image and first-order tube-to-chord transport consume native continuous piecewise position/first-derivative enclosures across actual carrier knots. Local rank separation may recenter the same Cartesian coordinate functionals inside original homogeneous coefficients before quotient evaluation; a constant origin changes no derivative and supplies no sampled authority. These first-jet proofs do not grant C1, Hessian-Taylor or normal authority. UV-curve Taylor bounds retain their separate strict knot-partitioned jets; full-triangle smoothness/regularity and normal checks remain independent.

For continuous first jets, component interpolation error is bounded by maximum vertex residual plus `span_u * width(du) / 4 + span_v * width(dv) / 4`, then the outward Euclidean norm. Mathematical derivative-interval midpoints cancel barycentrically; weighted mean absolute coordinate deviation is at most half its span. Rank remains independently proved. Only an insufficient ordinary bound/rank requests the existing Jet2/smooth-span path; native errors and cancellation remain terminal.

When the ordinary smooth Taylor bound rejects, the same completed whole-box Hessian may also bound variance on the actual UV triangle. Per component, Young's inequality with one positive finite constant `r` gives `A = diag(Muu + |Muv|*r, Mvv + |Muv|/r)`. The barycentric remainder is at most half `max_i |Ui-c|_A²` for any finite center `c`; choose one longest-metric-edge midpoint, with outward metric/residual arithmetic and charged scans. Rounded center/edge selection supplies no native authority. Take the minimum of complete scalar bounds, using fresh physical residuals and the actual original or auxiliary UV vertices. Invalid local arithmetic declines only this proposal; native failures stay terminal. Do not replace the original full-box smoothness proof or omit auxiliary transport.

A rejected triangle may use one auxiliary affine UV triangle strictly inside an independently qualified native smooth span, preserving physical vertices and original coverage/normals. Add complete original-box first-jet transport for all barycentric UV displacement. If that auxiliary comparison still rejects, its qualified Hessian may be tightened once by hulling four closed subboxes covering the same box, intersecting the old valid Hessian and recomputing the same triangle bound. Never apply this Hessian step across an unqualified C0 join. Fixed scratch is precharged; no heap, recursive partition, conditioning ladder, epsilon waiver or omitted transport is permitted.

Only after all existing bounds decline may one fixed filled-triangle proof subdivision use an already completed parent C1/Hessian proof. Exact symbolic midpoint children `[0,01,20]`, `[01,1,12]`, `[20,12,2]`, `[01,12,20]` cover the unchanged triangle. Outward UV midpoint intervals are intersected with endpoint-coordinate hulls and the parent box; three native F0 range queries enclose their source positions. Physical midpoints enclose `(Pi+Pj)/2` on the original affine plane, NEVER `F(midpoint)`. Apply the same rectangle Taylor remainder and interval vertex residual to each child, then take their maximum and the minimum with prior complete bounds. Auxiliary subdivision uses actual auxiliary UVs and original physical vertices, adding original-to-auxiliary transport once outside that maximum. Fixed scratch is precharged once; work/cancellation and terminal native failures remain mandatory. No rounded replacement mesh, recursion, new Hessian queries, dynamic cache or inferred C1 is permitted.
