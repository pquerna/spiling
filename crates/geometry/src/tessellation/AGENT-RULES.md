# SolidCore display conversion rules

Borrow the definition's single immutable BREP. Use the pinned `solidcore-mesh` edge/face candidate APIs and the deterministic query-local kernel context. Publish through `MeshChunkBuilder` in `Definition::face_ids()` order; the matching source STEP table and checked inverse ordinal mapping must remain complete. No native face may disappear, including an empty, failed or unresolved face. Do not replace a failed mesh with an empty artifact.

Candidate chord tolerances, normal estimates, convergence diagnostics and sampled deviation are not certificates. A returned candidate's convergence flag does not preempt the complete independent audits; only their certified shape-error exceedance requests refinement. Audit every published triangle against the actual stored native coefficient law in its natural UV chart with `parametric::DisplayTriangleProof`; include fresh physical vertex residuals. Analytics in `FaceInfo` are descriptions, not evaluator or parameter-correspondence authority. In particular, a candidate using an analytic position correction must still satisfy the same coefficient-based bound.

Independently prove positive oriented UV triangles, opposite interior-edge cancellation, simple boundary circuits and a complete original-loop/edge-interval bijection using native shared vertex provenance. Bound every whole native 3D trim interval against its published chord; where a native edgeuse has a UV expression, also bound its complete lifted carrier image. Missing original UV bindings may be proposed by native inversion only for display: no chart, face-domain or manufacturing receipt is created. Extra boundaries, holes omitted from the source-loop bijection, crossed circuits, duplicate triangles and ambiguous incidence refuse.

Use outward interval operations and same-span continuity evidence for Taylor remainders. An outward UV enclosure may be intersected with the natural carrier domain only after `curve2d_image_within_box` proves the complete expression stays there using native homogeneous coefficients. Never tolerance-clamp a chart or interpret STEP angles as native NURBS UV. Regular endpoint normals are checked against coefficient-based normal enclosures and explicit outward faceuse/region orientation. Singular endpoints/poles require an actual one-sided coefficient closure proof; nearby or centroid normals are not a substitute.

Exact display-box cache identity equates signed-zero endpoints only. Do not quantize nonzero UVs or rewrite native mesh coordinates to make captured and queried boxes agree.

Native trim displacement, including literal carrier lifts, is geometric, not an assumed affine match of curve and chord parameters. A projected proof requires complete literal C0, genuinely C1 closed leaves for second-order bounds, orthogonal displacement plus finite-segment endcaps, and original range-endpoint gaps. C0-only leaves use complete coefficient position images, never Taylor or derivative claims across a join. Lifts use the actual composed physical jets and coefficient-contained chart/carrier continuity. Those endpoint gaps and continuous projection prove chord-to-curve coverage too; point-to-segment distance alone is insufficient. This proof grants no UV parameter correspondence.

Keep carrier plus complete trim displacement at most 0.025 mm and independent packed reconstruction error at most 0.025 mm. Refinement is requested only by an independent shape-error audit, with at most eight finer candidates and one cumulative 20,000,000-work allowance. Audit workspace remains 32 MiB; actual native output capacities, proof tables, graph arrays and packed-builder capacities coexist and must be accounted. Native `QueryBudget` charges candidate work/cancellation and fallibly admits visible buffer growth before allocation, then checks actual capacity; debit its consumed work into the same display allowance even on a failed candidate. `EdgeSet`, `FaceMesh` and `Mesh` report actual retained visible backing storage. Opaque Spade CDT and kernel evaluator/cache allocations are separately host-bounded under native candidate hard counts, not claimed as exact logical owned bytes or an enforced RSS peak. Never turn a query limit into a partial face or geometric fallback.

Spade constraint-result vectors are opaque during the dependency call: the native bridge makes only an explicitly provisional minimum reservation, never a guessed output-capacity upper bound. Immediately on return, actual `Vec::capacity()` is reconciled before any handle processing or coexisting adapter growth; excess refuses with a typed resource error and drops the result. Returned vectors are fully visible/charged thereafter. The opaque in-call allocation peak remains in the separate host-resource boundary.

Candidate failures retain the original source, face ordinal, attempt and cumulative/native-query work. A terminal failure after refinement also retains the first independent shape-error cause; do not infer a carrier-domain or mesher failure from an unscoped work-limit diagnostic.

The integration owner exercises the owning display tests and unchanged housing/fitting consumers. A typed display failure preserves admitted native source geometry and does not create manufacturing authority.
