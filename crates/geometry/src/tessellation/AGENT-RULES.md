# Strict native display conversion rules

Mesh admitted faces through the pinned public trim-aware compressed-shell mesher, preserving every retained original per-use UV curve, source-face order and orientation; never discard those charts for fresh plain-shell reprojection. Borrow UV curves and carriers, precharge all temporary snapshot/view metadata, and release carrier guards before audit preparation. Reject absent/empty/non-triangle faces. Initial tolerance is one quarter of the 0.025 mm native display budget, triangles and two search trials. At most eight finer shape-error candidates share unchanged work, byte and mesh caps. Regular UV triangles use whole-domain outward native jets and interpolation remainders; the same barycentric transport pairs every triangle point with the complete source UV-triangle image and conversely. Trim and quantization remain independent gates. No redundant infinite-analytic-carrier guard, mesher tolerance or vertex-only curved check is a certificate.

`FaceInfo.geometry` is descriptive; native chart/trim evidence remains mandatory, including representation-equivalent splines and sweeps. Cached orientation is relative to its regular canonical carrier, not the kernel topology flag. Derive analytic normals from that carrier/sign, or use retained native orientation for generic carriers, and validate against native normals plus topology orientation. Inward hole normals remain inward. Reject malformed/nonfinite attributes/indices, nonunit normals, degenerate triangles, reversed winding and unresolved/singular charts rather than repairing them.

Before packing, prove UV triangle-chain orientation and interior-edge cancellation, simple boundary circuits, complete ordered native coedge interval correspondence, uniform material winding and one-to-one coverage of every native/mesh trim loop. Interval UV curve jets, knot partitions, shared native joint uncertainty and whole-coedge source-to-rounded-chart margins bound complete trim displacement; expand the surface derivative query over the complete source tube, not just nominal coefficients. Nearest-parameter searches only seed correspondence. `mesh-trimmed-mm-0.05-v2` records carrier and trim errors independently, with their sum ≤0.025 mm. Share one cumulative work allowance across faces, intervals and triangles. Remap only the current face into contracts' identity-free chunk builder; identical position/normal values may share a vertex. Float32 displacement is separately ≤0.025 mm and carrier-plus-trim-plus-quantization ≤0.05 mm. Enforce narrowed budgets; discard partial output on cancellation/error. Never mint session/artifact identities.

Exercise `cargo test --locked -p spiling-geometry` and the real native corpus smoke; see [geometry evidence](../../../../docs/quality/geometry-evidence.md).
