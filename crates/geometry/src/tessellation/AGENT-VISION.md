# SolidCore display conversion vision

Produce disposable, bounded, source-face-mapped display meshes from one immutable native definition. The packed result belongs to the downstream display consumer; native geometry does not invent sessions, artifact identities, original parameter bindings or manufacturing domains.

Ordinary display uses the explicitly tolerant preview profile: complete face meshing, measured approximation, closed oriented incidence and independently checked packed buffers. It does not inherit exact coefficient certification or grant material authority. The trimmed profile remains a separate exact diagnostic consumer rather than a prerequisite for seeing an admitted part.

`solidcore-tess` plans the reader's own record: every face in its own (u, v), every edge sampled once so neighbouring faces share boundary nodes bit for bit, with its statistics naming what it refused, filled leniently or limited. Spiling only names those nodes and faces, and charges and admits the plan's work and memory. Independent coefficient bounds and topology audits decide publication; the planner's own statistics never do, and they are reported rather than silently accepted.

Import availability does not imply display certification: unresolved singular normals, crossed or degenerate charts, missing source incidence and exhausted work or bytes remain typed display failures, leaving the immutable source admitted.
