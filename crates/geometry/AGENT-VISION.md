# Native geometry boundary vision

Provide one authoritative immutable native BREP boundary for import, inspection and manufacturing queries. Preserve source face/coedge identity and exact parameter correspondence independently of analytic classification. Directional extrema return uncertainty brackets with feasible trimmed-domain witnesses; height/slab queries separate critical events, regularity, native shape fidelity and occupied envelopes. Geometry evidence comes from bounded interval evaluation and native topology, never display samples or transformed AABB contact guesses. Engine composition owns sessions, publication, worker supervision and artifact lifetimes.

Expose an immutable native full-height vertical rectangular wall certificate for source-backed surface-finish consumers. The finite source-local origin, canonical horizontal phase direction, outward supporting normal and exact width/Z span derive from the original face use, bounded line edges and analytic extrema. This narrow proof is not a textured BREP, global extrusion certificate or physical surface qualification; manufacturing owns posed/process eligibility and additive construction. Original and independent box sides plus cylinder/top/recess/partial-height/sloped/identity regressions define the geometry gate; unexecuted tests do not establish acceptance.

Expose a separate immutable full-height vertical cylinder certificate with source-local XY center, radius and analytic Z range. Generic admitted face/coedge analytic references preserve source attribution through rational curve conversion for future native consumers; cylinder admission proves physical rim cycles after paired seam cancellation, not a fixture-specific chart shape. Unchanged independent OCCT full-face cylinder and genuine original exported periodic sources exercise this lane; the frozen original split cylinder and full periodic inward bore remain refusals. No certificate implies manufactured grip or physical process qualification.

The source profile is `step-native-trimmed-v2`: practical bounded analytic, spline and sweep representations share one adapter, not one planner per family. [Native geometry parity](../../docs/adr/004-native-geometry-parity.md) specifies the operation gates; [geometry evidence](../../docs/quality/geometry-evidence.md) records exercised revisions and explicit limits. Exact conversion, display coverage, native intersections, directional bounds and slab fidelity remain separate obligations. A source family name or an unexecuted regression is not acceptance; unresolved conditioning, trims, singularities or continuation receive operation-specific refusals without losing native geometry or inventing certificates.

No general STEP hierarchy, project persistence, alternative kernel, mesh-derived section or manufacturing. Future consumers reuse this facade rather than introducing a second import or geometry service.
