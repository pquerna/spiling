# Geometry boundary vision

Provide one immutable adopted material source for import, inspection and manufacturing: SolidCore BREP for STEP, retained analytic/faceted complexes for STL/3MF. [ADR005](../../docs/adr/ADR005-native-first-development-geometry.md), [ADR012](../../docs/adr/012-tolerant-brep-curves-and-feature-heights.md) and [ADR013](../../docs/adr/013-hybrid-imported-material.md) own those boundaries. Preserve original provenance, units, shared topology and stable region identity. Recover useful analytic structure locally while retaining difficult residual geometry.

Source representations converge at shared preparation and material queries. Exact native certificates remain optional, operation-specific capabilities; descriptive analytic metadata never supplies their authority. Sections, events, occupancy, trim correspondence and physical process qualification retain distinct obligations and declared error policies. Engine composition owns sessions, publication and artifact lifetimes.

No alternate manufacturing planner, invisible shape-changing repair, project persistence or physical-readiness claim. [Geometry evidence](../../docs/quality/geometry-evidence.md) owns concrete consumer capabilities and refusals.
