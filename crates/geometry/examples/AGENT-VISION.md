# Native geometry examples: vision

Provide auditable original inputs, a real headless geometry facade surface and reproducible native-derived cross-language goldens. Shared recipes construct genuine closed cone frusta, regular sphere belts, ring tori, bounded polynomial/rational boxes and profile sweeps while preserving source topology and frozen analytic fixtures. Eighth-grid polynomial aliasing and denominator-zero sources challenge whole-domain certification rather than importer labels. Diagnostics report tight support brackets, canonical source/native faces, separate interpolation/trim/packing errors and native section numerics. Original operator prisms isolate nonintegral top, off-grid shelf and inward native slope. Facade/golden commands do not establish engine transfer, desktop rendering, assemblies or physical manufacturing; freeze actual measured support in corpus evidence, not successful construction or compilation.

Parity selectors also include the true full analytic sphere with original collapsed-pole topology, exact integer-homogeneous rational cylinders and doubly curved rational ring tori, an independently affine-parameterized torus source and a rotated trimmed Bezier sweep. Source-surface seam branches remain authored STEP PCURVEs rather than recovered nearest charts. Independent face-count, physical-bound and analytic section-area oracles are checked through the real facade.

The generator distinguishes `same-kernel-original-roundtrip` recipes from `independent-cad-exporter` sources. Existing OCCT STEP bytes retain their original manifest hashes and license sidecars; they are read unchanged, never regenerated or silently re-exported by the parity producer. Producing a new manifest exercises import, certified display, native sections and tight directional bounds but does not by itself prove separate engine/render/manufacturing acceptance.

Blind-pocket recipes sew the original outer top rim and reversed plug wall/floor topology into a real closed B-rep, including a representation-equivalent bounded rational floor. The floor at 4 mm is a genuine source-defined outgoing stratum: sections below it contain the full 20 mm square, while interior sections above it contain the square minus the radius-3 mm disk. Native slab/joint consumers must derive this behavior from the imported source, never manufacture a test-only definition.

Exact-only analytic STEP production is respected at source construction: generic revolution recipes declare full carriers directly from their original profile curves in coordinate-axis frames, with quadrant placement retained in genuine trim topology. Sphere/torus carriers use directly declared exact frames; the rotated trimmed sweep uses an exact signed-permutation quarter-turn. No rounded processor rotation is silently serialized as an identity chart, and arbitrary source frames are not rationalized merely to bypass a producer refusal.
