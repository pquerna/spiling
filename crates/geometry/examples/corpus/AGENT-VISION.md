# Original controlled AST recipe vision

Make original dimensional and adversarial inputs reproducible without third-party CAD or malformed string-splicing shortcuts. Reusable AST primitives support consumer-visible importer regressions, while generation-only recipes remain outside production geometry and test compilation when unused.

The recipes retain frozen AP203/AP214/unit/error sources and distinguish genuine representation-equivalent bounded NURBS geometry from invalid or unimplemented conversions. Native parity construction and end-to-end operations live in the shared `../parity/mod.rs` recipes. Controlled templates do not extend production STEP support or repair unsupported user geometry; measured operation-specific outcomes, not historical filenames or enum labels, determine acceptance.
