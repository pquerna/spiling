# Original controlled AST recipe vision

Make original dimensional and adversarial inputs reproducible without third-party CAD or malformed string-splicing shortcuts. Reusable AST primitives support consumer-visible importer regressions, while generation-only recipes remain outside production geometry and test compilation when unused.

The intended envelope is the frozen planar/cylindrical corpus and declared AP203/AP214/unit/error behavior. These templates do not extend production STEP support or repair unsupported user geometry.
