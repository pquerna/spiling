# Typed Cartesian backend rules

Emit only validated inert specifications and normalized paths. `DiagnosticBodyOnly` uses CartesianAbsoluteGcodeV1; `CompleteMarlin` is the complete single-extruder workflow in its printer's dialect (Marlin 2, Prusa Buddy, Klipper; the Bambu placeholder refuses emission with `unsupported_capability`); `FixedDualMarlin` uses Marlin2FixedDualGcodeV1. These are explicit workflows, not compatibility aliases. No connectors or qualification claims.

Every program establishes G21/G90/M82 (and G92 E0 outside fixed-dual). A complete job then renders its declared `lifecycle` steps in order through `lifecycle.rs`, the only place a typed step gets its dialect command form; opaque block lines are written verbatim after placeholder substitution, never interpreted. Prime and park are the lifecycle motions owned here: prime travels then deposits one bounded line; park lifts vertically from the actual realized endpoint to the highest emitted Z plus the declared clearance (capped at the envelope top, strictly above the last position and every emitted Z), then moves XY at that height. Track the highest emitted Z from actual rounded endpoints, never the plan. Marlin/Prusa lines over 95 bytes (MAX_CMD_SIZE) refuse. The pure realization stream owns ordered object/support entry, first-layer/bridge factors, M106 S0..255 cooling and conditional external retraction/Z-hop/recovery. No implicit process defaults, firmware G10/G11 or undeclared commands.

Complete-job assumptions: one Cartesian XYZ extruder, native unshifted coordinates after homing, no workspace/home offsets, tool offsets, skew, firmware retraction, mixing, volumetric limits, linear-advance extrusion modification or fixed G0 feed overrides. A bed mesh the lifecycle activates corrects Z beneath command space and is reported. Configuration and calibration records are named by hashes; these assertions are not measured hardware truth. Homing trajectories and opaque block motion are firmware-owned and excluded from command-space inspection/collision certification.

All interpolated moves carry XYZ and F (mm/min); deposition carries increasing absolute E (filament mm). Extruding commands (deposition, arcs, priming, waste) are bounded by the printer's print feed; travel, lifecycle motion and E-only retraction by its separate travel feed. On a printer declaring arc moves, a planned arc run is one G2/G3 in the XY plane (Marlin's default; no G17-G19) with I/J from the actual start to the projected center; its E is the commanded arc length times the bead area, and its swept bounds must stay in the envelope. Retract/unretract is E-only G1 with explicit F at known stationary XYZ; decreasing E may be negative and exact recovery contributes no deposited material. XYZ/F precision is six decimals, E eight, for every current dialect (`OutputDialect::precision`). Cooling uses ordinary default print fan M106 S0..255 for the next queued motion, without LASER_SYNCHRONOUS_M106_M107; configuration declarations must admit external E-only retraction. Thermal targets/limits are finite integral Celsius degrees, rendered as integers. No physical fan/temperature truth is inferred.

The fixed-dual branch pins the reviewed Marlin configuration and two independently fed coplanar hotends. Establish addressed flow/M218 XYZ offsets and T0 S1 before native home/G92 E0. Later switches barrier, retract, lift, stage the incoming waste-lane carriage in the outgoing tip frame, barrier again, set standby, select Tn S1, reset E, wait addressed M109 R, repay exact incoming E8 debt, descend and deposit supported waste. Both active frames and carriage bounds must remain valid; never relocate the model or weaken the envelope to permit selection. S1 changes logical tip coordinates, not carriage position or feeder debt. One realization stream/safe plane owns motion; both tools own supported waste each fine layer; either model group may be empty. Backend independently bounds XYZ6/E8/feed/flow and conservative inactive-tip/body exclusions. Final retract/park/addressed shutdown are mandatory. Fixtures refuse; declarations are not physical collision qualification.
XYZ6 endpoint projection and physical-decimal bead enclosures belong solely to `contracts::manufacturing::program_coordinates`; backend owns formatting, verifier owns independent parsing and exact correspondence to the projected endpoint. Support contact is a minimum actual emitted full-bead gap, never nominal height plus epsilon. Unrepresentable coordinates refuse with `program_coordinate_conditioning_failure`.
Every rounded lifecycle/object endpoint remains in the specified command envelope; complete park Z must strictly clear all object/prime Z and remain representably higher after rounding. The complete priming segment must not touch/intersect the full all-layer XY deposition bounds expanded by half bead width, two XYZ6 half-steps and scale-aware arithmetic slack; check both specified and rounded endpoints. Protected holes, interior gaps or higher prime Z are not exemptions. This conservative command-space ownership exclusion is not toolhead collision certification; component shapes remain metadata.
The explicit current Cartesian XYZ6 output contract lives in `contracts::manufacturing::program_coordinates`; its `project_xyz6_coordinate_mm` is the sole endpoint projection used by the emitter and support proofs. Carry `xyz6_coordinate_error_mm` for remaining physical-decimal representation error, not geometry-policy slack. Future dialects must explicitly choose their coordinate contract. Clearance must certify actual projected endpoints, never independently repeat rounding or treat a Boolean work pad as a geometric enclosure. Shared encoding rules do not make emitter output an independent replay certificate.
The final nominal-program footprint gate projects both support/interface and object/Bridge endpoints through that contract, carries remaining decimal error on both sides, and uses each declared bead's full height. Native continuous-target exclusion cannot stand in for actual emitted bead clearance when bounded approximation or coordinate quantization extends M/D beyond true source C. The geometry capsule-pair predicate owns the full-segment distance proof. Every actual support/object pair must either prove the requested minimum vertical gap conservatively, in either direction, or prove unchanged XY capsule clearance; merely non-overlapping Z intervals do not discharge the requested gap. Actual selected support contacts additionally prove the declared Z gap as a conservative minimum after final raster/anchor changes; neither an exact physical attachment nor an exact requested gap is promised. Emitter/decoder agreement does not itself prove these properties or physical collision freedom.

Object/support nominal commanded volume is actual projected XYZ6 chord length times `(width-height)*height + PI*(height/2)^2` using each consumed edge width and physical interval height, with applicable flow factors exactly once. Never substitute recipe width, unrounded source chord or nominal reporting height. Divide by round filament area for absolute E; prime and recovery remain distinct. Independent replay decodes actual chords/E and separately checks source endpoint fidelity. Algebraic width-equivalent intervals retain actual parsing/subtraction uncertainty, not intended-accumulator E8 quantization bins; rounded circular nominal targets are admitted by nominal geometry plus forward E8 fidelity, without clamping inverse bounds or weakening process/gradient checks. Reject precision collapse, actual envelope/feed/flow/resource excess; poll cancellation and stream one bounded program. Public emit is not verification.
