# Typed Cartesian backend rules

Emit only validated inert specifications and normalized paths. `DiagnosticBodyOnly` uses CartesianAbsoluteGcodeV1; `CompleteMarlin` uses Marlin2SingleExtruderGcodeV1. They are distinct explicit workflows, not compatibility aliases. No macro strings, arbitrary firmware commands, connectors or qualification claims.

Both establish G21/G90/M82/G92 E0. Complete jobs additionally establish Celsius with M149 C, reset M200 D0, M220 S100, M221 S100 and M107; home all axes with G28; disable leveling with M420 S0; wait on bed M190 R and nozzle M109 R; position then prime with one bounded linear deposition. The pure realization stream owns ordered object/support entry, first-layer/bridge factors, M106 S0..255 cooling and conditional external retraction/Z-hop/recovery. Complete jobs finish with M400, vertical clearance lift from the actual realized endpoint, XY park, M400 and heater/fan/motor shutdown. No implicit process defaults, mesh leveling, firmware G10/G11 or user macros.

The reviewed [Marlin 2 contract](https://marlinfw.org/docs/gcode/G028.html) assumes one Cartesian XYZ extruder, native unshifted coordinates, no workspace/home offsets, tool offsets, skew, leveling, firmware retraction, mixing, volumetric limits, linear-advance extrusion modification or fixed G0 feed overrides. Configuration must support M200, M420 and M149 (`TEMPERATURE_UNITS_SUPPORT` enabled); no restoring/enabling leveling after homing. Configuration and calibration records are named by hashes; these assertions are not measured hardware truth. [M149 C](https://marlinfw.org/docs/gcode/M149.html) establishes Celsius rather than assuming inherited units; [M200 D0](https://marlinfw.org/docs/gcode/M200.html) selects filament-length E; [M190 R](https://marlinfw.org/docs/gcode/M190.html)/[M109 R](https://marlinfw.org/docs/gcode/M109.html) wait both directions; [M400](https://marlinfw.org/docs/gcode/M400.html) completes queued motion before shutdown. Homing trajectories are firmware-owned and excluded from command-space inspection/collision certification.

All interpolated moves carry XYZ and F (mm/min); deposition carries increasing absolute E (filament mm). Retract/unretract is E-only G1 with explicit F at known stationary XYZ; decreasing E may be negative and exact recovery contributes no deposited material. XYZ/F precision is six decimals, E eight. Cooling uses ordinary default print fan M106 S0..255 for the next queued motion, without LASER_SYNCHRONOUS_M106_M107; configuration declarations must admit external E-only retraction. Thermal targets/limits are finite integral Celsius degrees, matching firmware integer targets. No physical fan/temperature truth is inferred.
Every rounded lifecycle/object endpoint remains in the specified command envelope; complete park Z must strictly clear all object/prime Z and remain representably higher after rounding. The complete priming segment must not touch/intersect the full all-layer XY deposition bounds expanded by half bead width, two XYZ6 half-steps and scale-aware arithmetic slack; check both specified and rounded endpoints. Protected holes, interior gaps or higher prime Z are not exemptions. This conservative command-space ownership exclusion is not toolhead collision certification; component shapes remain metadata.

Object/support nominal volume is length times `(width-height)*height + PI*(height/2)^2` using true scheduled thickness and applicable flow factors. Divide by round filament area for absolute E; prime and recovery remain distinct. Reject precision collapse, actual envelope/feed/flow/resource excess; poll cancellation and stream into one bounded program. Public emit is not verification: compiler mandates independent decoding. Artifacts explicitly lack physical qualification.
