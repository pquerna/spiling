# Planar planner vision

Provide one deterministic, bounded native-section planner with revision-captured actionable metadata preflight. A true-distance layer schedule retains exact top, horizontal native feature and support-stop events instead of forcing integral top or grid-aligned shelves. Sloped planar and tilted cylindrical native carriers are consumed only through the existing original native intersection/degeneracy/topology gates. Analytic projection extrema certify placed bed/top limits without promoting rotated control bounds to exact contact.

Immutable material ownership remains separate from path generation: walls, physical top/bottom skins, solid or alternating rectilinear sparse interior, protected holes and nominal intentional void. Offsets operate on boundary neighborhoods rather than independently shifted finite edges, so disappearing supports do not invent material islands; construction primitives do not replace authoritative source rings. Certified extrusion slabs share footprints and current wall layouts; varying geometry/height/skin horizons cannot reuse an invalid template. Bridge process roles come from bounded gaps in actual previous nominal deposited beads, including roofs above sparse void, not from full material domains.

Breakaway support is deliberately vertical and bounded: genuine floating/cantilever demand must have a useful fixed-XY corridor from bed to contact, excluded from source object and protected holes with declared XY/Z clearances. Support/interface paths, support-only lower layers and explicit bounded zero-deposition contact intervals retain separate object/support area and nominal material accounting. Blocked components, unresolved thin features, collapsed requested walls, unsupported native surfaces and exhausted numerical/resource gates refuse with actionable diagnostics instead of repair or silent erasure.

Acceptance requires original native fixtures, public compilation and independent emitted-program replay. The inward planar ramp, non-grid shelf/top and real shared rigid box/hole assemblies provide reproducible numerical software gates. Density, nominal bead footprints, bridge limits and process declarations are not physical strength, swept-bead collision, firmware qualification or printability guarantees. General STEP/freeform, mesh repair, branching supports and physical printer control remain outside this lane.
