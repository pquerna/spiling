# Planar planner vision

Provide one deterministic bounded native-section planner with captured admitted/refused/unresolved preflight. One actual-height schedule combines true native events/top, field and support events, then certified native/field refinement instead of grid-forced shelves or midpoint proof claims. Geometry owns placed bounds and height/metric conversion; native slab feature/occupancy certificates authorize each ordinary manufacturing operand, not surface names.
Closed endpoint contacts refer to actual adjacent source-M leaves, including settled successor candidates. A bounded ordered geometry ledger resolves every accepted source contribution/final-M hash before path classification; independent occurrence fidelity does not imply composed-union boundary/incidence. Streamed native results serve actual source construction once, with a bounded iterative dependency frontier instead of a discarded sampling pass or unbounded recursive lookahead. Persist separate accepted proof-scope counts and actual achieved error only after the complete stream resolves.

Immutable material ownership remains separate from path generation: walls, physical top/bottom skins, solid or alternating rectilinear sparse interior, protected holes and nominal intentional void. Offsets operate on boundary neighborhoods rather than independently shifted finite edges, so disappearing supports do not invent material islands; construction primitives do not replace authoritative source rings. Certified extrusion slabs share footprints and current wall layouts; varying geometry/height/skin horizons cannot reuse an invalid template. Bridge process roles come from bounded gaps in actual previous nominal deposited beads, including roofs above sparse void, not from full material domains.
Repeated physical horizons reuse raw final-D intersections through one bounded immutable range cache, preserving actual adjacent footprints and protected holes. Current fill-bead clearance and reconciled wall/residual ownership apply only after material survival; neighbor walls cannot become fictitious voids. Cache geometry, clearance staging and cumulative work remain within ordinary limits. Initial ownership and support-regenerated raster choices consume the same final-D authority, never native-slab shortcuts.
Optional cached intersections are reclaimable without weakening geometric or work limits. Support regeneration stays within an explicitly frozen retained-cache reservation; source material and live query operands are never hidden by eviction.

Persistent whole-face planar/cylindrical ribs and mapped image relief contribute bounded source-local outward material before ordinary ownership, while Smooth reveals the original source. Certified rectangular supporting chains and complete exterior cylinders retain their actual native sampled boundaries, protected holes, unchanged BREP and separate original/realized/added coverage. Shared scalar fields and robust domain proofs support carrier-specific correspondence without a second planner or finish-specific emitter. Images vary in U and source Z; non-grid row events and local vertical refinement prevent midpoint aliasing. Native M slabs stay distinct from realized D span identities throughout walls, skin/fill horizons, actual deposited anchors and both support rasters. First-wall sampled image resolution is attributed and advisory, not universal feature-survival or physical accuracy. Partial/freeform carriers, material painting, random jitter and physical tactile certification remain outside the acceptance envelope.

The planner separates requested D from required material for first-wall coverage without generating a second erosion. Exactly attributed image islands require C: their original M plus all non-image finish additions, including ribs on other faces of the same island; other islands require full D. C is cached per native slab and shares M for image-only occurrences. Fine requested image loss remains advisory, never permission to omit native or rib material, relax reachability thresholds or invent deposited anchors.

Breakaway support is deliberately vertical and bounded: genuine floating/cantilever demand must have a useful fixed-XY corridor from bed to contact, excluded from source object and protected holes with declared XY/Z clearances. Support/interface paths, support-only lower layers and explicit bounded zero-deposition contact intervals retain separate object/support area and nominal material accounting. Blocked components, unresolved thin features, collapsed requested walls, unsupported native surfaces and exhausted numerical/resource gates refuse with actionable diagnostics instead of repair or silent erasure.

Acceptance exercises original primitive/spline native fixtures through the same walls/fill/skins/supports, public compilation, emitted bytes and fresh independent replay, plus explicit conditioning/topology/process/resource refusal. Density, nominal beads, bridge limits and declarations are not physical strength, collision, firmware or printability proof. General source representation does not authorize unsupported native operations, mesh repair, arbitrary finishes, branching support or physical printer control.
