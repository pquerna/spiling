# Planar planner vision

Provide one deterministic native-section planner for bed-anchored integral-height solids and repeated rigid placements. An immutable planar ownership seam separates native material classification from path generation: walls, physically requested top/bottom skins, solid or deterministic rectilinear sparse interior, protected holes and nominal intentional void. Durable per-layer area/volume accounting accompanies ordinary kind-labelled paths without retaining a second full polygon plan. Certified extrusion slabs reuse native footprints, share the current immutable wall/fill layout across skin phases, and reuse stable classification/parity templates without changing layer Z, local skin horizons, topology or resource accounting.

The support envelope is deliberately conservative: world vertical planar/cylindrical carriers, layer-aligned horizontal planes, fixed integral top layers, no generated supports or bridging, no erased thin regions, no collapsed requested walls and no hole/island topology change under wall offset. Neighboring footprints classify local roofs and floors conservatively across shrinking slab transitions; overlapping skins have one owner. Placement rotation is evaluated through transformed native carrier geometry, not rejected by pose name. Sloped planes, tilted cylinders and non-layer-aligned horizontal features are explicitly unsupported. Output describes nominal bead geometry, requested sparse fraction and actual-path nominal material volume only. Density is not a strength or physical-printability claim, and independent emitted replay—not region metadata—accounts the realized software output.
