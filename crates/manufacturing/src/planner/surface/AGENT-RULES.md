Resolve captured ordered definition layers before occurrence layers; last enabled whole-face fill wins, and Smooth has no geometric construction. Exact native face identity and `VerticalWallFace` / `VerticalCylinderFace` certification are mandatory. Only upright bed-anchored invariant native extrusions enter this lane; source BREP is never edited. Share allocation-free periodic scalar profile values and conservative first/second derivative bounds. Planar protected-end tapers remain domain envelopes. Construct constant-source-Z outward ribs with total 0.001 mm construction bound: chord error ≤0.00099 mm plus source/frame/phase/pose/directed rounding ≤0.00001 mm, independent of native/offset budgets. Planar sampling includes both ramp breakpoints and native parameters. Cylindrical q(t) lifts each actual native edge radially; conditioned L/d derivative bounds, original endpoints and waveform peaks determine subdivision. Never replace native material by an analytic circle. Admit sample/proof/retained/segment work before growth.

Match one unique contiguous monotone full-span native exterior sample chain using the immutable face certificate, both trimmed endpoints, supporting side and material-left winding; support the closed-ring seam and never associate by plane proximity alone. Prove positive inward overlap and no native boundary/hole entering its collar. Anchor merged profile intervals to actual native segments; robust outward orientation, progression, material inclusion and nonintersection prove M union A. Original active-face samples may become interior material rather than D boundary vertices; every original material point remains included, while untouched/protected vertices, endpoints and hole rings remain exact. Never use a floating Boolean to rewrite unrelated source rings.

Cylindrical ownership requires exactly one convex positively oriented full-turn native exterior ring, a strictly interior axis and the unchanged 0.005 mm radial native envelope. The axis need not lie in material: preserve every hole ring exactly. Source-local +X/right-handed local-Z phase survives rigid pose, including flipped Z; exact cyclic closure copies the first stored output point. Every chord owns one native-edge interval and rounded native vertices satisfy both adjacent native supports. Shared robust simplicity, exact source-vertex inclusion and no proper/entering-contact retention predicates prove the closed source remains included. Count/phase are structural controls; manufacturing additionally requires pitch ≥ four beads, depth >0.022 mm and ≤half a bead, and attributed ordinary first-wall radial peak within its separate0.011 mm budget.

Directed rounding applies only to new samples, fits the fixed construction reserve and never tolerates missing M. Refuse unsafe widths, lost additions, topology changes, excessive coordinate precision/work or expanded-envelope/occurrence/component/prime exclusions.

All wall/fill/skin caches, actual-stroke bridge/support regeneration and clearance use the same realized material D. Finished occurrences require strictly separated XY bounds in overlapping Z interiors; append their native-preserving rings without re-overlaying them. Ordinary untextured material retains its existing union path. Source area remains separate from D and added area; geometric added volume is not deposited extrusion or tactile efficacy. Acceptance requires native compile, exact native retention/phase/chord-bound regressions and independent actual-byte replay through the [CLI/workbench workflow](../../../../../docs/quality/development.md#source-face-vertical-ribs); never claim edited BREP, physical grip or printer qualification.
