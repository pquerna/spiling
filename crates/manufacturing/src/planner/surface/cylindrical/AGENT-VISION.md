Realize a complete certified exterior cylinder's durable source-local periodic field as additive native-preserving closed material and ordinary first-wall motion, without changing BREP or downstream planning interfaces. Native subdivision, chart/ring seam, carrier parameterization, phase and rigid pose including flipped Z must not define the requested scalar field.

Shared allocation-free scalar profiles and bounded robust material proofs are reusable across current planar and cylindrical consumers. This module retains the genuinely cylindrical correspondence, positive-axis conditioning and radial lifting mathematics instead of inventing a generic plugin or separate safety/planner stack. Exact holes and original/realized/added accounting remain inspectable.

Acceptance uses unchanged independent full-cylinder import, actual compile and independent emitted-byte replay, alongside synthetic unequal-radius native-ring/chord/hole/seam and resource/cancellation invariants. Partial/split/inward/recessed carriers, varying-Z/helical/crossed relief and physical grip/printability claims remain outside the current certificate envelope. Wider envelopes require real correspondence/retention/anchor proofs, not guessed eligibility from reusable helpers.
