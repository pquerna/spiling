Thin/unreachable native M, lost source/holes/topology and missing/ambiguous native-to-C/D ownership refuse. Required operand assembly copies no polygon coordinates; count extra C staging, retained points and work, and release M/C after their coverage/diagnostic consumers finish, before support.
