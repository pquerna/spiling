# Planar planner rules

**Memory rule.** The goal is never running a reasonable machine out of memory ([ADR009](../../../../docs/adr/009-resource-envelope-policy.md)), not avoiding caches. Indexes, caches, memos and data carried across steps or schedule retries are allowed when their bytes are admitted against the live working set, they are shared through owners or borrows rather than copied per consumer, and they are released when their last consumer is done. Size them by what a step actually needs (per layer, per span, per distinct material), not by the whole job when streaming would do; never key them so they grow without bound.

Consume immutable native definitions and rigid occurrences through geometry's placed world-height facade. Geometry alone normalizes frames and certifies pose/metric conversion; manufacturing consumes actual world-millimetre loops without duplicating spline/pose mathematics. Native outer/hole classification remains authoritative; associate holes with their smallest containing outer before union, canonicalize rings and never substitute source-name geometry, a second kernel or source mutation. When the exact native path refuses on geometry or proof-work grounds and the job's features allow it, `plan` retries the whole job in [ADR012](../../../../docs/adr/012-tolerant-brep-curves-and-feature-heights.md) tolerant mode (`tolerant.rs`): sections from the same BREP's own tessellation within the profile tolerance, simplified within its remaining budget, feature heights merged within the printer's Z motion resolution, classic constant-width perimeters, `PreflightEvidence::Tolerant` and no native certificate. Exact-mode rules below apply only to the exact path; tolerant mode refuses enabled finishes and fixed-dual routing rather than dropping them; its supports consume source-facet closed-slab occupied projections and all-height protected holes through `tolerant::project_protected_voids`. Raw boundary scopes and approximation enclosures remain distinct from exact native certificates; sampled sections alone supply no continuous clearance.

Native rectangular/full-turn exterior cylinder certification and conservative expanded-envelope/occurrence/component/prime metadata are not section/offset proof. Carrier branches share scalar fields and robust bounded material-retention/simplicity proofs. The constrained upright bed-anchored extrusion lane retains strict rib first-wall checks; image first-wall feature loss is reported through bounded attributed advisory witnesses without weakening field, retention, XY chord, vertical-error, cache or anchor invariants. Retain native0.005/offset0.005 and separate finish0.001 mm budgets; no source-name recognition, native re-export, mesh/UV replacement, untextured fallback or second production R/E pipeline.

Release old plan/output/permission capacities before rebuilding, retain cumulative work, and qualify every cap's real extrusion without new overlap or coverage credit.

Object offsets preserve material/holes as the exact Minkowski sum/difference with one circumscribed convex disk polygon (at most0.0025mm outward disk error within the0.005mm offset policy): one overlay of each ring's convolution cycle with that disk, positive winding for dilation and negative winding of the reversed rings for per-island erosion, with retained-point and byte admission before growth. Only rings below one-thousandth-tolerance-squared area (integer snap residue) are dropped, and only holes of a dilation or islands of an erosion. This is the same set as material plus/minus the union of per-edge disk capsules; no sampled, one-sided or tolerance-merged shortcut. Never hull/simplify material or drop positive islands. Hatches stay in perimeter-owned classified domains; only proved Bridge anchor extensions may leave their owner within the same hole-split positive-fill row. Bridge admission uses previous actual nominal deposited strokes, not the full native source material. Intersect each candidate centerline analytically with the union of exact segment capsules at declared half-bead radius, using perpendicular strip and endpoint-circle intervals; no polygonized-circle or overlay approximation certifies an anchor. Interior anchors require positive-length intervals, not isolated tangent points. Two-ended spans must be no longer than `process.max_bridge_span_mm`; only bounded half-bead one-sided overhangs are tolerated without support. Contours containing a bridge remain whole closed contours with Bridge process role. With support disabled, unanchored/over-limit demand prints as authored and its span is recorded in `unsupported_overhang_mm` on that layer, as other slicers print overhangs without support. Complete construction and actual-anchor/clearance proofs are rebuilt; proximity grants no support authority. Reuse the native slab pool and cumulative allowance, preserve all event endpoints and process minima, and fully requalify the completed schedule. Only actual deposited-anchor failure authorizes these pair proposals; source occupancy and arbitrary geometry errors do not.

Never remove/move contour points or flatten curved runs. A successful startup probe may move its first wall plan into full admission only when it already captured the complete native stream and there is no material construction. Schedule retries reuse only bit-identical work. After a complete-pass support refinement, tolerant lanes first admit a schedule truncated a few layers above the refined index under the probe's horizon rules but complete-pass support semantics: a refinement it finds is the complete pass's first one; no decision, any error, or a protected void claimed only by unseen higher demands that can reach a corridor falls through to the complete pass. Within one lane, tolerant protected-void projections persist per occurrence. Nothing that survives a retry may be keyed by output index, which a split shifts: survival ranges are keyed by their complete input description (course positions and schedule identities) and per-layer support admission by schedule identity, so both are reused above a split wherever their inputs are unchanged. Clipped demand corridors cross every lower layer and persist only below the first changed one. These records follow the memory rule; optional ones are dropped when they do not fit, never refused.

Cathedral (`cathedral.rs`, `region/web_roof.rs`) is general: one part-level lattice frame, per-layer ceilings from section differences, corbels closing under each ceiling by its own height. Grid and Cathedral rows are whole constructions: bridge recovery and row coalescing never rewrite them, and a stroke left unsupported is reported on its layer. The verifier mirrors the generator from each layer's recorded frame and ceilings; change both together.
