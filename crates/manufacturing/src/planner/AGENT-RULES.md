# Planar planner rules

**Memory rule.** The goal is never running a reasonable machine out of memory ([ADR009](../../../../docs/adr/009-resource-envelope-policy.md)), not avoiding caches. Indexes, caches, memos and data carried across steps or schedule retries are allowed when their bytes are admitted against the live working set, they are shared through owners or borrows rather than copied per consumer, and they are released when their last consumer is done. Size them by what a step actually needs (per layer, per span, per distinct material), not by the whole job when streaming would do; never key them so they grow without bound. The live allowance is the host-selected `crate::working_set` (`bytes()`/`units()`), installed once per compile/preflight/verification; never compare against a fixed byte constant or add a per-site ceiling. Work moved to another thread re-enters the captured allowance with `working_set::scope`, and concurrent tasks admit a share of it, not the whole.

`plan` is the one slicing pipeline: every job, STEP or hybrid, slices in [ADR012](../../../../docs/adr/012-tolerant-brep-curves-and-feature-heights.md) tolerant mode (`tolerant.rs`). Sections come from the same BREP's own tessellation within the profile tolerance, simplified within its remaining budget after each ring is started at its least vertex; feature heights merge within the printer's Z motion resolution; section walls are classic constant-width perimeters; the plan records `PreflightEvidence::Tolerant` and no native certificate. There is no exact native lane and no fallback retry; never reintroduce one. Consume occurrences through their placed tessellations; geometry alone normalizes frames and pose. Associate holes with their smallest containing outer before union and canonicalize rings; never substitute source-name geometry, a second kernel or source mutation. Sampled sections alone supply no continuous clearance: supports consume source-facet closed-slab occupied projections and all-height protected holes (`tolerant::project_protected_voids`).

A span holds its first layer's section over each following layer whose own section agrees with it within the profile tolerance, compared against the held section so error never accumulates; mapped image finishes keep one-layer spans for their per-layer vertical-error check. Consumers that need one construction over several layers (permanent plate extents, combined bands via `same_prism`, fixed-dual material domains via `material_runs`) rely on that hold or on an explicit within-tolerance comparison, never on bit identity across a re-sampled curve. Full-height material for a plate or band is `tolerant::inner_material_admits`: the section minus the projection of every facet crossing the interval's interior, padded by the support enclosure, with flat faces within one Z step of either end bounding it. Never accept a plate or band from sampled endpoints or outer occupancy.

[ADR005](../../../../docs/adr/ADR005-native-first-development-geometry.md) is active native-first DEVELOPMENT authority. The imported BREP (or retained hybrid surface complex) is the one source of M, sectioned within tolerance; original STEP retains identity/units/finish operands. Source/void/anchor/command gates remain independent, without a second material reconstruction or transferred historical qualification. Fixed-dual source reservations add ownership, never replace the occurrence union.

Resolve captured ordered whole-face finish layers through exact native face identities and the sole image metadata/content table, including disabled references. Native-preserving additive construction D precedes occurrence union, span/cache identity, walls/fill/skins, actual anchors, regeneration and support clearance; native M remains separate for source/added accounting and native-edge diagnostic correspondence. Native rectangular/full-turn exterior cylinder certification and conservative expanded-envelope/occurrence/component/prime metadata are not section/offset proof. Carrier branches share scalar fields and robust bounded material-retention/simplicity proofs. The constrained upright bed-anchored extrusion lane retains strict rib first-wall checks; image first-wall feature loss is reported through bounded attributed advisory witnesses without weakening field, retention, XY chord, vertical-error, cache or anchor invariants. Retain native0.005/offset0.005 and separate finish0.001 mm budgets; no source-name recognition, native re-export, mesh/UV replacement, untextured fallback or second production R/E pipeline.

The skeleton `WallPlan` generator (`plan_material_domain_walls`, fixed-dual material domains only) borrows requested final D and a distinct mandatory native/C operand. Its independent required band is D minus the captured nominal contour-depth erosion, never candidate swept output. The bounded segment graph proposes source-associated constant/per-edge bead commands; actual commanded inner/outer occupancy separately qualifies underfill, original exterior/holes, local arm connectivity, predeclared junction/count excess and independent XYZ6 uniqueness. Reconcile actual outer wall ownership out of the nominal core before fill/skin consumers. Only certified image-owned islands substitute C, retaining original M plus every non-image addition; other islands retain full D. Attribute original M/C/D ownership independently, never by polygon order. Widths/physical intervals feed first-wall finish/rib proof, actual anchors, support and H1. Semantic command counts and actual owned/staging byte budgets remain separate. Finite boundary-lane/cap proposals retain the same source graph and requested band; only UnsupportedGeometry advances construction. Release old plan/output/permission capacities before rebuilding, retain cumulative work, and qualify every cap's real extrusion without new overlap or coverage credit.

`preflight(input,cancel)` captures revision and admits explicit intent/source/pose/placement from the placed tessellations. It does not realize a schedule. At most 256 required/Compile-only checks preserve admitted/refused/unresolved evidence; truncation never restores admission. Conservative bounds are placement exclusions, not bed/top witnesses or deposition events.

Construct one bounded actual-height schedule from merged feature heights, mapped rows, source material-cycle boundaries and support/interface stops. Uniform whole-occurrence cycles reuse optical LayerCycle but bind immutable loaded materials; insert physical boundaries before balancing, never recolor by mutable layer index or run a second scheduler. Balance mandatory intervals below nominal height; mapped-field refinement retains the same schedule. Caller-specified wall intervals still qualify unchanged. Refuse unresolved neighborhoods and process-minimum intervals; the layer count has no cap and is admitted only against the working set (`working_set::records`). Never invent roots or erase terminal material. Never widen the strict bead-height limit.
Breakaway stop construction follows the complete feature/field event set and encloses actual Cartesian XYZ6 projection, decimal error and height arithmetic before selecting a support/interface partition. Image row events additionally propose post-event minimum-gap floors before balancing, separating native-anchor startup from later contact demand without claiming either anchor. Deduplicate these bounded proposals; never recursively derive new stops from them. True source/field events are immutable; when a proposed stop/floor is too close to retain a printable interval, keep those events and use the latest existing support top that proves the requested minimum. Contact selection and the final actual-path obligation share the explicit program-coordinate contract, with no geometric epsilon, second slicer, coordinate cache or fabricated contact. Larger actual gaps are honest minimum-policy outcomes, not exact physical attachments.
Append source-Z density/plate edges before global balancing, never by cutting an already fine grid. Split effective process/D/classification dependencies at those events while borrowing the held M. A permanent plate needs full-height inner material over its whole filled owner plus bead/XYZ6 radius; metadata alone must refuse. Typed coverage owns the exact construction region, not an authored density1 override. Final inward XY construction keeps row/phase/identity and raw Z; no lost/split/sub-printable row, search or tolerance relaxation. Reprove final actual plate/upper landing and separate wall attachment, stop H1 at event neighborhoods, and charge construction area/actual volume once.

Continuous-target exclusion alone does not cover an accepted M/D prism or nominal emitted bead outside true target C. Require the separate shared actual-capsule pair gate after final support/object raster and anchor decisions: actual XYZ6 support/interface versus all actual object/Bridge capsules, both radii and decimal endpoint enclosures, full bead Z and unchanged XY gap. Skip a pair only when its conservative vertical separation, in either direction, meets the requested minimum Z gap; otherwise require the full XY capsule clearance even for non-overlapping bead intervals. Expand bounded schedule searches by that Z gap without reprojecting already enclosed heights. Exact touch with zero requested Z gap may satisfy the vertical minimum. Source C/void gates remain independent. The same scalar vertical enclosure is computed from all actual deposition endpoints; binary scope selection and borrowed path scans may use indexes under the memory rule below. This is nominal-program footprint evidence only, not physical machine collision qualification.
Overlapping physical skin horizons share one bounded job-local range cache of raw final `SectionSpan.material` D. Intersect material over the complete actual-distance horizon first, qualify the current fill bead afterward, then apply the current wall/residual ownership in classification. Neighbor wall-owned material is not void; a wall-policy change alone cannot create false floors/roofs. Native M/C identities grant no D reuse. Attribute range overlays, final fill clearance and initial/support-regenerated neighborhood construction to the cumulative planar work counter (counted for cancellation, never capped). Account cached ranges, borrowed leaves and live query temporaries without double-counting shared owners; retain the same authority through support regeneration.

Reclaim unpinned least-recently-used neighborhood geometry under combined retention pressure before refusing optional cache overhead. Live shared operands remain counted; charge all eviction scans/recomputation and admit owned temporary aggregates. Freeze cache-retention capacity before support reserves its staging pool, so regeneration cannot grow unaccounted cached geometry.
Classified ownership reports all currently live scratch to the same reclaiming retention authority. Post-support raster requests additionally account for live support/interface/protected-domain geometry; the frozen cache pool does not cover newly owned query or classification temporaries. Count mandatory geometry, pinned cache operands and live scratch exactly once.

Object offsets preserve material/holes as the exact Minkowski sum/difference with one circumscribed convex disk polygon (at most0.0025mm outward disk error within the0.005mm offset policy): one overlay of each ring's convolution cycle with that disk, positive winding for dilation and negative winding of the reversed rings for per-island erosion, with retained-point and byte admission before growth. Only rings below one-thousandth-tolerance-squared area (integer snap residue) are dropped, and only holes of a dilation or islands of an erosion. This is the same set as material plus/minus the union of per-edge disk capsules; no sampled, one-sided or tolerance-merged shortcut. Never hull/simplify material or drop positive islands. Hatches stay in perimeter-owned classified domains; only proved Bridge anchor extensions may leave their owner within the same hole-split positive-fill row. Explicit path width and physical upper-minus-lower height drive stadium area, pitch, capsules, E and role-flow volume, never nominal layer count or clamped reporting height. `combined` starts from a valid fine alternative, borrows retained paths and stages bounded coarse paths/landing overrides before committing once. Full-band inner material, final-D/finish invariance, source/profile identity, skin/events, actual band-base support and fine printing-time stability remain separate; future coarse cannot support earlier fine walls. Only explicit proved local vetoes retain fine; hatch/anchor errors, indeterminate work, resource and cancellation propagate. Preserve fine separators and strict nominal core bonds independently from outer overlap consent. See [combined-band contract](../../../../docs/protocol/manufacturing.md#combined-sparse-bands), not a second scheduler or proof framework.
Conservative Boolean work admission may tighten a coarse ring-product estimate through the shared [edge sweep](boolean/AGENT-RULES.md). Preserve allocation-free coarse-safe calls; account input/index construction, cancellable sort/scans and all intra-/cross-operand candidates, including pinned-overlay integer-conversion uncertainty. Work is counted for cancellation and accounting, never capped (ADR009), independent of source coordinates, native/offset/finish tolerances and protected holes.

Wall qualification may retain immutable full-D/nominal Boolean admission metadata and one permission-loop workspace, never kernel geometry authority. Keep full original operands/order and repeated kernel conversion/sort/candidate charges. Persistent workspace capacity remains live through every permission stage, including raw fallbacks; helpers that admit it internally receive an outside receipt excluding those same bytes. Release prepared metadata and workspace before final permission union.

Bridge admission uses previous actual nominal deposited strokes, not the full native source material. Intersect each candidate centerline analytically with the union of exact segment capsules at declared half-bead radius, using perpendicular strip and endpoint-circle intervals; no polygonized-circle or overlay approximation certifies an anchor. Interior anchors require positive-length intervals, not isolated tangent points. Separately certify the actual candidate endpoint disk's positive-area overlap with a previous nominal capsule by strict point-to-segment distance below the sum of declared radii; equal-distance tangency has zero area and is not an anchor. Endpoint certification never dilates interior capsules or shortens measured centerline gaps. Two-ended spans must be no longer than `process.max_bridge_span_mm`; only bounded half-bead one-sided overhangs are tolerated without support. Contours containing a bridge remain whole closed contours with Bridge process role. With support disabled, unanchored/over-limit demand prints as authored and its span is recorded in `unsupported_overhang_mm` on that layer, as other slicers print overhangs without support. Before full admission, a bounded actual-stroke startup probe may split the original first physical pair, retaining its frozen upper endpoint through retries; enabled support probes its physical unreachable-contact gap and can retry a finer local pair for blocked-corridor demand near previous strokes with nominal endpoint overlap. Complete construction and actual-anchor/clearance proofs are rebuilt; proximity grants no support authority. Preserve all event endpoints and process minima, and fully requalify the completed schedule. Only actual deposited-anchor failure authorizes these pair proposals; source occupancy and arbitrary geometry errors do not.

Bridge admission traverses maximal straight sampled strokes, including across closed seams, using only cross-product arithmetic-roundoff collinearity. Never remove/move contour points or flatten curved runs. Tiny fully unanchored strokes still demand support; the half-bead overhang allowance needs an actual positive-length interval or positive-area endpoint anchor. Exact intervals and endpoint certificates retain provenance: ordinary contact-resolved deposition keeps its object role. Explicit demand-rooted orthogonal construction may consume a genuine bounded two-ended gap with original eligible lower contacts as Bridge, without promoting those contacts to direct previous material; fully supported/tiny spans remain ordinary.

Ordinary hatch resolves the authored build-XY axis from the layer's hatch course (`LayerElevation.course`, published as `PrintLayer.hatch_course`), never its output index: each base-schedule layer is its own course and every later split keeps its parent's. The alternation key (`hatch_key`; `hatch_alternation_keys` on plans) is the course outside refinement and counts down from the top of each maximal run of split-course layers, so a run alternates internally, the course above still crosses its top and no layer above the run changes. Template reuse keys that axis within final-D/classification/height dependencies. Orthogonal recovery stages the same borrowed classified role polygon/density/holes and selects only independently genuine bounded Bridge rows under the preceding provenance rule. Each finite selected run must intersect original actual demand in XY. Full hatch cells replace only their exact original owner; actual merged bead-width anchor corridors reserve cross-role extrusion without erasing unrelated dense skin cells. Clip original authored strokes to the complement: never rehatched or recentered lattices, ordinal authority across reconstructed domains, fully supported rows relabeled Bridge, or layer-wide ordinary overrides. Merge coincident same-row Bridge pieces once through existing positive-fill/actual-anchor authority, then freshly prove all final Bridge paths and the full mixed candidate. After support changes actual lower/contact strokes, reconstruct rather than retain a stale patch. Charge original/alternative/ownership/interval buffers, live anchor capacities and work before growth. Support raster retains separate alternating admission. Whole-row joins clip authoritative positive fill at actual classified coordinates; endpoint extensions stay inside the same hole-split row and use nearest direct previous-stroke anchors, except unanimously mandatory-skin orthogonal demand may also reach original eligible lower contacts under the support module's strict construction rule. Never fill internal sparse gaps, add unrequested rows, use source-material anchors or relax physical tolerances. All actual extra anchor extrusion enters segment/volume/replay accounting; nominal role area and requested density are not exact actual porosity.

Breakaway support groups proved actual-stroke demand per contact layer. The current realized D supplies a corridor candidate only after demand is proved, never an anchor map. Clip the fixed-XY candidate against every crossed continuous-target outer C piece plus finish bounds and height-scoped protected voids. Offset/Boolean work allowances only propose this candidate. After final raster alternatives and actual-anchor regeneration, the planner must prove every actual emitted Support/SupportInterface capsule outside the raw certified C and relevant positive void masks at unchanged nominal bead radius/XY gap, including full bead height and physical-decimal/f64 endpoint enclosure. No mask/scope truncation or sampled-point substitute is allowed. Centers remain half a bead inside candidates; support hatches enter at wall centers for positive bead overlap instead of tangent-only starts. Only bed-anchored vertical support is claimed; highest support Z stays below contact by the declared gap and interface thickness/density are explicit. Revalidate every demanded object edge against actual emitted contact strokes so another valid column cannot certify a blocked component. Report separate support area/volume and never label intentional contact-gap object deposition a two-ended free bridge.

`exposure.rs` marks a mandatory TopSkin path `exposed` after the layer's paths are final, when the next scheduled layer's section D does not cover every point and edge of it; covered top skin stays TopSkin (role, owner, axis, checks) and only takes the solid speed. Exposure is a speed declaration, never classification.

Classification reuse binds material/required owners, physical horizons, exact interval height and construction zones—not hatch direction. Path templates additionally bind both hatch axes and phase. Drop templates with stale top direction before phase/path admission; retaining classification must not retain obsolete path buffers.

A successful startup probe may move its first wall plan into full admission only when there is no material construction. Preserve the exact D/C cache key and first interval/sample; release all Arc aliases before handoff. Failed attempts, classifications and anchor results never transfer. The transferred wall becomes the ordinary first cache entry with its existing point, payload and index accounting, and the plan equals the one built after releasing it.
Schedule retries reuse only bit-identical work. After a complete-pass support refinement, tolerant lanes first admit a schedule truncated a few layers above the refined index under the probe's horizon rules but complete-pass support semantics: a refinement it finds is the complete pass's first one; no decision, any error, or a protected void claimed only by unseen higher demands that can reach a corridor falls through to the complete pass. Within one lane, tolerant protected-void projections persist per occurrence. Nothing that survives a retry may be keyed by output index, which a split shifts: survival ranges are keyed by their complete input description (course positions and schedule identities) and per-layer support admission by schedule identity, so both are reused above a split wherever their inputs are unchanged. Clipped demand corridors cross every lower layer and persist only below the first changed one. These records follow the memory rule; optional ones are dropped when they do not fit, never refused.

Check cancellation around section/Boolean/buffer work and during every layer/path/hatch/anchor edge. Bound section+wall+classified retained points, corridor/interface/protected/temporary contours, combined anchor interval/gap records, cumulative support/skin Boolean and actual stroke-pair clipping work, schedule layers, raster rows and combined object/support deposition segments against the working set before growth; never against a fixed count. Borrow prior paths and reuse one bounded interval workspace; do not polygonize anchor circles or copy full path arrays to filter roles.

Exercise `cargo test --locked -p spiling-manufacturing`; operator fixtures are immutable source bytes checked by `node tools/verify-fixtures.mjs`, not regenerated by a legacy kernel. Tests include non-grid scheduling, inward planar ramp, tilted cylinder, sparse roof bridges, mixed closed bridge contours, cantilever/floating support, protected holes, blocked components, cancellation and bounded demand. Focus changed consumer properties rather than repeating unchanged slow cases; actual CLI/workbench compilation and independent replay remain required. Software behavior never establishes physical printability.

Cathedral (`cathedral.rs`, `region/web_roof.rs`) is general: one part-level lattice frame, per-layer ceilings from section differences, corbels closing under each ceiling by its own height. Grid and Cathedral rows are whole constructions: bridge recovery and row coalescing never rewrite them, and a stroke left unsupported is reported on its layer. The verifier mirrors the generator from each layer's recorded frame and ceilings; change both together.
