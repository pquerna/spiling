# Planar planner rules

Consume immutable native definitions and explicit rigid occurrences; inverse-transform world horizontal planes, then transform native mm loop points into world XY. Native outer/hole classification is authoritative. Associate holes with their smallest containing outer before union; canonicalize ring starts/orientation and polygon order. Never replace sections with tessellation or source-name-specific geometry.

Admit only world vertical planar/cylindrical carriers and layer-grid-aligned horizontal planar carriers. Diagnose sloped planes and nonvertical cylinder axes as unsupported. This makes each interior section exhaustive within its fixed layer: sub-layer horizontal shelves cannot be silently missed.

Fixed layer height is bed-anchored: output Z is bed+(index+1)*height, conceptual sample Z is the interior midpoint. Global layer quantization is at most 1e-6 mm; horizontal-face alignment also requires height-relative tolerance `min(1e-6 mm,height*1e-6)`. Reject nonintegral global top heights rather than truncating or silently adding partial layers. Empty per-occurrence sections are valid. Empty aggregate layers or new material outside the previous section buffered by twice native sampling tolerance are unsupported supports.

Reuse a native XY section/union only in certified open extrusion slabs between horizontal faces when transformed carriers have exactly zero nonvertical residuals. Otherwise sample every layer natively. Retain each footprint once per slab and check support at every transition. Cache one immutable wall/fill layout for the current span independently of skin-neighborhood keys; share it into classifications without copying its polygons. Include native footprints, cached walls/fill and current classified domains in retained point admission. Drop prior classification before allocating its replacement and drop the layout on span changes. Reuse parity path templates only while the current footprint and both physical-skin neighborhood span keys remain identical. Charge every actual layer's retained segments before copying paths. Conceptual midpoint metadata is not a native-query log.

An immutable `ClassifiedRegions` seam owns wall centers and disjoint bottom-skin, top-skin and interior domains. Physical skins cover `ceil(requested thickness / layer height)` layers, conservatively intersecting all neighboring native footprints; missing neighbors expose a skin and locally shrinking sections expose local tops. Bottom ownership wins overlapping skins. Native holes are excluded throughout; serialized layer coverage records protected-hole area separately from material area. Wall, bottom, top, solid-interior and sparse-interior areas partition material area. Frame, millimetre units, material and source/occurrence provenance belong to captured bundle intent/input; classification cannot invent new ownership.

Round offsets use a sagitta-derived maximum chord/radius parameter at 0.005 mm tolerance. Erode disconnected islands independently. Do not discard numerical fragments. Reject component/hole topology loss in requested wall centers and any region unreachable from first-wall centers after conservative miter dilation (0.01 mm tolerance). Interior hatch endpoints stop at the perimeter-owned erosion boundary, half a bead from the final wall center; do not extend strokes across walls or holes. Solid skins and 100% interior use nominal bead-area/layer-height spacing. Sparse interior uses that spacing divided by finite requested fraction in (0,1], deterministic centered rows and alternating X/Y axes. Intentional void is the nominal unfilled sparse-area fraction, not a polygon repair or physical coverage/strength assertion. Nominal deposition volume must be calculated from actual emitted path lengths, rounded bead area and flow multiplier; independent emitted replay remains mandatory.

Check cancellation around native/Boolean/buffer calls and during every layer/path/hatch edge. Bound input/output point counts, cumulative skin-neighborhood Boolean work, hatch rows, layers and retained deposition segments before growth. Native upstream calls are nonpreemptible and do not imply allocator-level RSS guarantees. Focused owning tests exercise holes/islands, skin overlap/local tops, sparse axis parity, emitted-length accounting, thin ribs, cancellation and bounded path admission; parent-owned native compilation and independent replay integration must also pass.
