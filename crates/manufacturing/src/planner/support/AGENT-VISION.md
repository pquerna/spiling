# Deposited-anchor bridge and breakaway support vision

Realize useful software-only supports for genuine floating/cantilever material while exposing real sparse-roof unsupported spans. Nominal previous deposited beads, not source-material existence, distinguish bounded two-ended bridges from unanchored demand. Bed-anchored fixed-XY corridors and actual contact-path revalidation prevent blocked components from acquiring another component's support certificate.

Realized D ownership, protected holes and source BREP remain untouched by supports; support/interface area and emitted nominal material are separate. The same native-plus-finish D feeds clearance and every regenerated object raster without changing original/added accounting. Explicit support-only lower layers and bounded empty contact intervals preserve true deposited bead heights. Numerical/topology/resource failures refuse rather than silently erasing demand or claiming branching support. Density, contact gap and nominal footprints establish no physical strength, collisions, machine qualification or printer readiness; native public compile plus independent replay is the acceptance surface.

One physical support center domain survives changing interface assignments. Interface is a centerline role and density requirement, not a separately eroded solid whose disappearance can erase lower deposited anchors. Preserve physical outer/hole walls, single ownership/material accounting, and true prior-stroke admission across role transitions.

Support regeneration reports its simultaneously live geometry to the ordinary planner's staging authority. Cache reclamation cannot hide support/protected domains or allow temporary object ownership to overrun the combined resource envelope.
