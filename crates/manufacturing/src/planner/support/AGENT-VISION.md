# Deposited-anchor bridge and breakaway support vision

Realize useful software-only supports for genuine floating/cantilever material while exposing real sparse-roof unsupported spans. Nominal previous deposited beads, not source-material existence, distinguish bounded two-ended bridges from unanchored demand. Bed-anchored fixed-XY corridors and actual contact-path revalidation prevent blocked components from acquiring another component's support certificate.

Object ownership, protected holes and source BREP remain untouched; support/interface area and emitted nominal material are separate. Explicit support-only lower layers and bounded empty contact intervals preserve true deposited bead heights. Numerical/topology/resource failures refuse rather than silently erasing demand or claiming branching support. Density, contact gap and nominal footprints establish no physical strength, collisions, machine qualification or printer readiness; native public compile plus independent replay is the acceptance surface.
