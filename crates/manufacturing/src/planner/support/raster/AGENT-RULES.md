# Support centerline realization rules

Own the private physical-support centerline and process-role realization consumed by the parent support planner. Inputs are one layer's kept support cells S and interface cells I from the sweep's conservative grid (`../grid.rs`), the recipe and the actual scheduled layer. Never access native M, image parameters, schedule construction, object ownership or support polygons here.

Erode S once on the grid by the declared half-bead width: E keeps only cells whose closed distance to every cell outside S exceeds it, so every nominal bead centred anywhere in E stays inside S. Interface is a centerline ownership/density selector, not an independently cleared solid: hatch ownership is E ∩ I versus E − I. Interface uses full density; ordinary support retains declared density. Beads may overlap a role seam; each emitted path belongs to exactly one role and is accounted once. A sub-bead I strip must not erase the physical support wall or create artificial clearance.

Hatch rows come straight from the grid: each lattice line's road segments are the runs of E (or E ∩ I, E − I) along it, at the line's exact coordinate, with run ends at cell edges clipped inward to cover XYZ6 projection; a line within that clip of a cell boundary uses only cells set on both sides.

Where an exact exclusion bounds E, walls and row ends sit on a sub-cell offset of it, not the staircase. The parent feeds the exclusions the sweep subtracted on that layer (`sweep::Exclusions`: occupancy, voids, nominal scopes, the top sheet; never carried prism holes) into `../field.rs`, a 1-Lipschitz clearance field over the boundary band. A wall sample moves outward toward its nearest exclusion by `F − level`; at a corner it may follow that offset to a second exact exclusion, re-proving the move. Total displacement stays within `reach` (below half a bead, so the centre stays in S). A row end that leaves E extends along its line to the first exclusion's offset, staying in S and short of its neighbour. A sample farther than `reach` above the level is seed-, ground- or role-bounded and keeps its grid position. Walls and row ends use one level on every layer: half a bead plus the XYZ6 endpoint enclosure plus 0.001 mm. Each staged edge that leaves E carries the largest bead radius its emitted XYZ6 capsule clears in the field (`WallReceipt::certified`, row ends split off as their own edges); final clearance accepts an escaping bead whose key and radius match instead of re-proving it, and proves every other escape exactly. Field queries are lower bounds by construction (bucket floors, sampled segment buckets), never optimistic. Every kept chord touching a moved point must keep the bead's exact clearance in the field; a failing sample pair on one edge gains its placed midpoint, otherwise reverts to the grid answer; changed rings are simplified by exactly checked chords and joined by positive winding. Relieved roof-access layers take no field. The independent verifier is unaffected.

Straight interface faces away from exact exclusions may recover less than half a cell of erosion rounding only when the shifted bead and both transitions stay completely in kept support. An interface rail on an exact offset can end at a second exclusion beyond the sample displacement radius only when the fitted turn clears exactly and its full centerline stays in kept support. Neither construction grants new cells or bypasses anchor checks.

Support hatches use a common build-frame lattice (line k at k · pitch) and pitch derived from captured resolved nominal layer height and actual bead width; clipping, islands, interface changes and thin layers never move a row. Odd lattice lines run reversed. Rows shorter than one XYZ6 command are omitted.

Elsewhere walls follow E's own traced boundary, simplified within one cell by chords that stay inside the closed E cells (never the extra-eroded contour: walls must reach E's edge to anchor object strokes beside protected voids), each edge split only where its cells' interface membership changes, judged at each piece's midpoint; same-role pieces continue one path with no seam outline. Fit sub-command pieces within 0.001 mm of every original span inside that contour, retaining endpoints, closure, width, physical interval and role boundaries; where no complete admissible chain exists, keep the admissible chords covering the most length and return the omitted length for `support_omitted_wall_mm`. Never invent a chord to avoid omission.

The parent uses only the layer's preferred axis (`HatchAxis::for_policy(AlternatingXY, hatch_key)`); what it cannot anchor is omitted and reported, never retried on the other axis. Build one lower inventory per layer and reuse it for every anchor query there. Preserve independently rooted straight wall runs across a closed seam; an unrelated failed path cannot erase rooted deposition. Lower/contact sets stay immutable during retention; same-layer paths never supply roots. Only SupportInterface may start on a separated part contact. Preserve capsule, endpoint, bridge and complete-clearance rules, and propagate errors/cancellation.

Byte-admit grids, contours, staged paths and scratch against the remaining geometry allowance; count raster, fitting and anchor work and check cancellation per row/edge. Borrow previous strokes; retain no alternate candidates. Software admission does not establish physical strength or printer readiness.
