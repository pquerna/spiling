# Deposited-anchor bridge and breakaway support rules

Own actual nominal deposited-footprint bridge classification and bounded vertical breakaway realization inside the native planner. Native object partition/source identity is immutable. Previous full material domains are never support certificates for sparse interiors. Intersect each candidate centerline analytically with the union of previous actual stroke capsules: declared half-bead radius, exact perpendicular strip and endpoint-circle intervals, with no polygonized-circle or overlay tolerance expansion. Interior anchors require positive-length intervals; isolated tangent points do not suffice. Separately certify positive-area overlap of the actual candidate endpoint disk and previous nominal capsule iff exact point-to-segment distance is strictly below the sum of their declared radii. Equal-distance tangency has zero area. This endpoint proof does not dilate interior capsules, add material or shorten uncovered centerline spans. Bound every two-ended free span using the sole `process.max_bridge_span_mm`; only half-bead-sized one-sided nominal overhangs are tolerated without support. Preserve closed contours under whole-contour Bridge role and account actual bridge/first-layer flow separately from support.

Native line sampling must not destroy continuous anchoring. Analyze maximal straight runs across contour seams with roundoff-only collinearity while preserving all original points; curved samples remain separate. Fully unanchored tiny strokes never inherit the half-bead overhang allowance. Capsule intervals and actual endpoint-area certificates separate direct previous-layer anchors from intentionally lower interface contacts. Ordinary contact-resolved deposition retains its object role. Explicit demand-rooted orthogonal construction may also use a nontrivial bounded uncovered span between two certified endpoint anchors from the original eligible contact set, retaining contact provenance and consuming Bridge process. During subsequent authored-axis merge checks, preserve that consumed Bridge role only under the same fresh complete actual gap proof used by the independent stroke analyzer; the changed merge axis neither erases prior construction nor supplies an anchor receipt. Fully supported, tiny and one-sided-admissible spans remain ordinary; never fabricate direct anchors.

Ordinary hatch never silently rotates. After authored-axis joins, stage orthogonal rows from the same borrowed classified role polygon/density; select only genuinely Bridge-certified rows in finite runs bound to original actual XY demand. For hole-split rows, a genuine demand-bound Bridge interval may survive independently of supported siblings; replace only its actual bead corridor, never their full row cell. Full original row cells replace only an entirely Bridge-certified owner; preserve required skin priority outside the actual merged Bridge bead-width anchor corridor. Analytically clip all affected ordinary strokes to exact cell/corridor complements without rehatched/recentered lattices. Merge coincident Bridge rows once and freshly prove every final Bridge path, then the complete mixed candidate; reconstruct after support changes actual lower/contact paths. No fully supported alternative row inherits another row's exception. Support raster retains its own alternating-axis admission. Whole-row joins preserve all required intervals and positive hole-split fill ownership. Connect only strictly overlapping actual XYZ6 current-row footprints; tangency is not connectivity or a previous anchor. Extend unsupported group ends only to nearest direct previous-stroke capsule anchors, bounded by neighboring required/accepted groups. Extensions into unrequired intervals retain a strictly positive actual footprint gap, including earlier extensions; a neighboring group's boundary cannot authorize filling the whole missing interval. Use physical-decimal/subtraction enclosures, never raw decoded-float equality or widened tolerances. Connectors are accounted Bridge geometry, not source anchors, support or dense-fill fallback.
Check the authored normal component directly, without constructing or coalescing whole-row corridors first: that would mutate its coordinates and demand set before alternative patch selection. Prefer the existing orthogonal recovery candidate whenever its complete proof succeeds. Only actual remaining mandatory-skin demand may stage separately constructed same-axis original skin rows using the existing whole-row/nearest-lower authority; retain only independently genuine Bridge rows and re-prove the complete candidate. Mandatory-only demand must not depend on an unrelated regional Bridge already existing, while fully supported normal jobs keep their cheap path. The explicit RegionalSkinBridge rule consumes only genuine direct regional demand; it never mutates the original component.

A supported one-ended orthogonal overhang is not Bridge authority. Only while resolving original authored-axis demand may the existing whole-row construction extend it to the nearest direct lower capsule. A unanimously mandatory-skin core in that orthogonal construction may additionally reach the original eligible lower Support/SupportInterface capsules inside the same whole-fill, hole-split row. Contact provenance stays lower contact, never direct previous material. Preserve the required core, source/void corridor and actual dimensions; select only after fresh nontrivial bounded two-ended union-capsule Bridge proof and the complete mixed-candidate gate. Same-axis, regional/mixed-owner and ordinary construction retain direct-only extension. This adds no contact eligibility or Z/XY-clearance allowance; final physical-gap, actual-anchor and independent replay obligations remain unchanged.

`DepositionPath.owner` retains immutable MandatorySkin or Regional(index) purpose through conversion, clipping and reraster. Ordinary merges keep only unanimous nonempty core ownership; unknown/mixed cores grant neither authority. One explicit three-point RegionalSkinBridge may join a demanded regional endpoint across adjacent already-required skin to the nearest real previous capsule. Preserve the regional core/cadence, the required skin lattice and source-owner domain; remove only exact coincident commanded subsegments, not offset/orthogonal skin for presumed bead coverage. Finite necessary join overlap remains explicit and all extrusion is charged. Its middle point binds the sole transition; whole-stroke lower support, both XYZ6 domain portions and packed per-segment association are independently verified. Another regional owner, separators, holes and ambiguous/multiple crossings remain excluded. SeparatorWall never enters Bridge coalescing or gains an anchor exemption.
Mixed endpoint construction stages each independently supported full regional row atomically with its patches and domain records. Remove only direct demands covered by that exact original row core; preserve every ineligible or ambiguous row's demand for the existing alternative-band construction. A successful subset never marks the whole component supported. Final whole-candidate anchor, coverage, owner and duplicate verification still gates publication.
Once an actual Skin endpoint bond chooses a continuous path, subsequent row coalescing freezes that exact unordered XY segment with matching process role and mandatory origin. It remains a required barrier to neighboring extension, and full fresh actual-stroke lower-support/Bridge admission still inspects it without exemption.
Typed permanent-plate residual demands and first-upper-interface obligations refuse before temporary-support generation; ordinary unrelated demand keeps existing policy. Final plate coordinates require fresh actual base/later/upper support and wall/hole-wall contact proof. A future plate/coarse stroke, source occupancy or temporary scaffold is never substitute earlier-anchor/permanent-interface authority.

Group actual-stroke demand per contact layer; Off retains authored unsupported-span warnings. Bed corridors and exposed section-top differences propose same-material roots, never attachment authority. Share each exposed landing across demands, then retain raw continuous source/finish exclusion, protected holes, nominal bead clearance and minimum upper/lower contact gaps. Establishment is per centreline interval, stored as grid cells and built bottom-up once in the sweep's up pass (`established.rs`), never per road: first-layer roads are established whole; above, an ordinary road's centreline is proved where it lies in the cells of `L = E[l−1] ∪ S[l−1] ∪ S[contact top]` (S: kept support cells) or where an exact capsule interval meets a lower road's proved subspan. Donors are the retained proved subspans, never whole donor roads or their raw chords. Established cells are the layer's inward model footprint minus every cell an unproved centreline's bead touches, plus cells wholly inside one proved bead; no dilation grants material. Only centrelines meeting a model cell off `L` take exact work. A Bridge road chains when both ends are proved (cells or exact endpoint contact), but landings use only cells under ordinary roads; permitted unsupported spans never enter `E`. The sweep hands each part root's landing cells to raster retention and final reconciliation (met there with the regenerated ordinary footprint); do not recompute `E`. `E` is producer evidence, never exported as proof; retain only the running layer, its proved subspans and repeated-road footprints. Optional support with no established lower/contact root is omitted; final object demand remains reported. Recheck the dependency chain after object regeneration. Count masks and all live support/interface/protected owners before admitting anchor growth, including replacement buffers. Physical S is eroded once; interface I changes role/density, not material boundaries. See the [manufacturing contract](../../../../../docs/protocol/manufacturing.md) for construction, replay and physical-qualification boundaries.
Support never asks the schedule for a thinner layer; slopes are thinned before planning. An unanchored wall stroke is ordinary support demand. A demand with no contact floor below the Z gap or no certified corridor prints as a reported overhang.
Contact selection compares outward actual XYZ6 full-bead height enclosures, never nominal `floor-gap+epsilon`. The same real selected contact layers drive interface roles, final actual-stroke anchor revalidation and a mandatory final emitted minimum-Z-gap obligation after all regeneration. No absent/empty contact path is evidence. Emit-safe support/interface partitions reserve only coordinate representation and construction arithmetic; retain true native/field events if a proposed partition would create a sub-process sliver, then select the latest existing provably safe support top. That can require a larger physical gap or quantized interface extent; never claim exact attachment or silently loosen the requested minimum. Generation may shrink higher footprints but must not lower/remap the original eligible contact top to manufacture anchor authority. Reuse the existing bounded schedule/contact storage and charge searches/final path scans to shared support work.
Continuous outer occupancy retains independent raw polygon/height-scope pairs, even when XY pieces overlap. Native scope is the closed slab intersected with that occurrence's certified placed-native Z outer brackets; equal endpoint sheets remain present. Whole-native finish eligibility proves exactly zero actual world-Z horizontal/normal/radial displacement, allowing the same captured native Z outer bracket; neither a family/upright label nor rounded nominal frame endpoints supplies this proof. Support regions come from one layer-major sweep (`sweep.rs`) on the conservative support grid (`grid.rs`), never per-demand or per-root columns or polygon Booleans. The cell size `SUPPORT_CELL_MM` is the recorded ADR012 support tolerance; one cell frame covers the demands' seeds. Every grid answer rounds to the safe side: seeds, occupancy, voids and clearance rasterize outward (every touched cell, dilations by exact capsule rows), landings and road footprints inward (cells entirely inside). Down, carry the union of demands' buffered target D from each contact top and subtract each layer's occupancy (pieces whose height scope meets that layer's bead slab, from its own and adjacent spans, collected once per span), protected voids and nothing else. Up, keep only what stands on the bed, on kept support below, or on a part landing's established landing cells of that object layer (local anchoring), then subtract nominal and bead clearance. Each layer's clearance is built once and shared by every demand; relief into one roof void is a separate bed-only channel. Never XOR overlapping raw masks. Kept support and interface leave the sweep as cells; raster draws rows and walls from them (see `raster/AGENT-RULES.md`). Only roof-access columns become polygons, traced from the grid eroded by one cell and simplified within one cell, so they stay inside the kept cells. Screens may only skip pieces that cannot change the result. Count raster work and byte-admit every grid. Final capsule proof selects applicable layers with charged schedule searches and actual full-height/XYZ6 extensions, never an all-layer rescan per polygon. These masks remain exclusion operands, not material accounting, anchors or physical collision truth.

Offset allowances, XYZ6 reserves and pinned-overlay work padding only construct candidate corridors; they never certify that a final Boolean mask contains raw continuous C or a protected void. Final clearance is an assertion on the sweep's grid, not a second search: the sweep hands its kept cells per layer (with the height band its exclusions hold for) and each nominal scope's excluded bead edges to `certify_support_clearance`, byte-admitted until consumed. Every actual non-model bead, at the emitter's sole XYZ6 projection with its endpoint error and full vertical extent, is rasterized outward on that frame; cells all kept means clear by construction. Only escaped cells (outside the kept set or the frame, or a bead outside its layer's band) take geometry's exact capsule proof, and only against the occupied pieces, protected voids and object beads whose bounds come within the gap of those cells; count them in the trace. A void a relieved channel enters keeps its full proof, which builds the withdrawal. Object beads the sweep never excluded (regenerated beads outside D, or a section whose band grew) are proved directly against support within the Z gap. Never add a fallback to the old exhaustive scans. Encode each forbidden polygon lazily once per proof; any unproved gap stays a typed refusal, not collision truth.
True-target C exclusion is separate from nominal-program footprint clearance: a bounded-error M/D prism, quantized union or actual bead can extend outside native target bounds. During placement, borrow the same sampled final-D owners and their actual XYZ6 minimum object-bead floors as conservative outer prism exclusions; monotonically clip higher column portions under the shared unchanged Z/XY-clearance math. Preserve lower mergeable portions, original contact tops and all relevant object layers; charge metadata, masks and staging, then release them before raster regeneration. These prisms are not anchors and do not replace final actual proof. Actual object beads can reach outside their D (variable-width walls, Bridge construction): find once per section, through a byte-admitted boundary index released with the demand loop, the actual bead edges not inside D beyond a sub-reserve allowance, and subtract their circumscribed capsules at the same clearance distance. The column's top bead also touches the next section's closed occupancy sheet: clip that section's height-scoped pieces reaching the top bead's actual upper height from the top layer only, never the lower fixed-XY corridor (unscoped invariant D stays with the height-aware nominal exclusion). Final object-bead clearance uses the shared capsule-pair proof with both nominal radii, unchanged XY gap and both decimal endpoint enclosures; full bead height governs applicability and only a vertical separation meeting the minimum Z gap, above or below, skips the XY proof. This proves declared nominal program footprints, not hardware collision freedom.
Protected voids come from geometry's complete source-branch proof or ADR012's closed derived-facet/all-vertex-height inventory, never a union of scheduled hole samples. Tolerant source approximation reserves additional contact separation without weakening declared XY/Z gaps or final actual-program clearance. After joint acceptance, retain only compact opaque source/pose/query/flow captures and moved hole buffers; drop raw outer sections/proof trees. Project each XY-relevant original-source capture once only after real actual-stroke demand and enabled support, using the same job work budget and actual remaining native bytes. Keep positive outer masks separate with their certified world-height scopes. A bottom-open native coedge branch can protect below a floating part; a blind pocket with a closed material-facing floor cannot. Validate original source/pose/interval/sample, count projected masks in live support/raster retention, and release unused captures on no-demand paths. Conservative masks still grant no anchor or physical-collision authority.

Final object admission can keep an already successful admission only when the ordered immediate-lower paths (including Bridge annotations) are unchanged, there are no surviving separated contact strokes, and the current object paths were not joined for recovery. Final support roots still reconcile first. Any changed input requires reproof against surviving lower/contact roads; restore original roles just before that proof. The verifier remains independent.

Stream tolerant forbidden pieces from shared prepared source owners at placement and final actual-capsule proof; retain no per-layer expanded facet arrays. Native captured operands remain borrowed. Retained-budget accounting may conservatively count shared owners repeatedly, but never allocate an unbounded deduplication map. Support raster stages each layer once on its preferred alternating axis against actual prior-support admission, with one lower-stroke inventory per layer; there is no orthogonal retry. Retain only established support and report remaining object demand. Finalize roots in deposition order before regenerating each layer’s object roads: earlier object roads are already final, and only invalid optional support and its unrooted descendants may be pruned. Never remove required object roads. Reconstruct ownership/bond receipts and assign Bridge process against those surviving lower/contact roads, not before a later pruning pass. Recompute support/model volume, omitted wall length and worst object demand before complete clearance and independent replay; a discarded optional root must not veto unrelated rooted work.

Return separate support area/nominal volume; include support paths in combined segment admission. Borrow filtered prior path/point arrays and reuse one bounded interval workspace; retain no polygonized alternate anchor map or copied motion plan. Optional stroke-analysis screening (blocks of at most 16 contiguous paths with identical physical top and direct/contact provenance, then a uniform edge grid once queries repeat) only selects which borrowed edges reach the unchanged per-edge box and capsule proof, in original order; it still charges every skipped eligible edge pair at its original position. Malformed, zero-length or out-of-range paths are never screened or indexed, so ordinary ordered validation owns their errors. Admit both inventory capacities only alongside worst-case interval/gap growth from actual retained capacities, re-checked for every analysis, otherwise decline before allocation; release the inventory when an analysis would not admit it and at the end of its borrowed lower-set scope (one read-only raster scan, preallocated establishment layer, component or bridge-row coalescing scope). Charge native/corridor/interface/protected/temporary contours and combined interval/gap records against the remaining retained-geometry budget. Bound cumulative Boolean/projection and actual segment-pair intersection work, finite arithmetic and hatch rows; check cancellation during each stage/edge. Owning tests use genuine box/hole occurrences for cantilever, floating, mixed closed bridges, blocked-plus-valid components and clearance/bridge/gap refusal; pure mathematical regressions cover exact circular caps, sparse void, shrinking-wall anchors and positive support-wall entry. Independent emitted replay and software-only qualification remain mandatory upstream.

Raster regeneration receives the live additional support/interface/protected-domain retained-point count, excluding the caller's object and frozen cache pool. The caller must include it with live neighborhood operands and classified scratch; a frozen cache reservation does not cover additional owned query temporaries. Clear anchor workspace before invoking the callback, and never count the object/cache reservation twice.
Demand analysis runs once per layer in a single pass: there are no schedule retries, so no per-layer admission is recorded for replay. Stroke analysis judges each path independently against the committed lower layer and lists every unanchored path; per-path gates (permanent diaphragms and their upper landings) read that list instead of re-proving paths one at a time. The Off startup probe decides its pair without building scaffold; complete admission owns every construction and final proof.

Anchor queries accept paired world-millimetre endpoints and the candidate's actual bead width. Every borrowed previous/contact edge carries its own bead width: interior intervals use that previous capsule radius, while endpoint-area overlap uses the independent sum of radii. Direct anchors must end at the candidate's explicit physical floor; lower interface contacts remain distinct and never cross that floor. Required path dimensions survive clipping, reraster and ownership-preserving Bridge construction; recipe width is only a producer input. Whole-row admission groups its axis, retained-point accounting and borrowed recipe, while cancellation and cumulative work remain caller-owned. Shared-union cache entries name a weak source owner and strong result; nearest-stroke diagnostics name distance in millimetres, deposition role and endpoints without copying path arrays.
