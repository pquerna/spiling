# Independent replay rules

Decode actual program bytes without importing backend formatting, parser or extrusion helpers. Validate bounded intent and normalized plan; consume expected path points through a borrowed iterator, never rebuild expected program/motion vectors. Persisted verified flags are not authority. Unknown macros/commands, duplicate/malformed fields, extra/missing/reordered modal/lifecycle operations and unsafe/mutated moves fail.

Diagnostic body-only sequence establishes G21/G90/M82/G92 E0 then required queued cooling and ordered path/process motions. Complete Marlin jobs additionally require exact ordered Celsius/volumetric/feed/flow/fan resets, native homing declaration, leveling-off, heating/cooling waits, explicit bounded prime and final barriers/lift/park/shutdown. Independently decode E-only stationary retraction, conditional travel/hop, exact unretraction and role/layer first-layer/bridge overrides; never call realization/emitter helpers. See the backend's reviewed Marlin assumptions.

Interpolated motions require XYZ/F; stationary E-only recovery requires known actual XYZ and feed, without invented XYZ movement. Deposition has positive absolute E deltas after exact recovery. Independently decode at most six XYZ decimal places, then require numeric correspondence to the explicit current Cartesian XYZ6 projection from the contracts program-coordinate module, for body and lifecycle motions alike. Raw-plan half-step/slack cannot authorize the alternate lattice neighbor, including negative half/zero ties. Remaining physical decimal representation error belongs to the geometric proof enclosure, not a second coordinate acceptance interval. F6 retains half-step0.0000005 plus scale-aware slack; E8 absolute0.000000005/delta0.00000001. The raw-to-projected XYZ half-step remains only a nominal-length/flow/report error bound, never endpoint admission. Plan tolerances never enlarge these bounds. Check actual endpoints, feed, decoded flow and queued fan PWM. Nominal object/support accounting uses true layer height and applicable factors; prime/recovered E contributes no object/support material. Independently reject actual prime intersecting conservative all-layer deposition XY bounds; no gap/hole/Z exemption or collision claim.

Unretraction restores the exact previously decoded pre-retraction E endpoint. Store that actual endpoint, not a boolean phase or the unrounded planned accumulator; nominal E rounding/arithmetic allowances never relax exact recovery or let report-only and packed inspection disagree.

One optional SPLR schema3 sink receives only accepted actual f64 XYZ/E/F/source lines. Kinds0..7 retain meanings;8 bridge,9 support,10 interface,11 retract,12 unretract. Stationary process records carry actual known coincident XYZ and real layer; fan state has no fictional motion record. Header96/record48, process_count at88, at most300004 events/16MiB; packed deposition_count includes object/support, report separates both. Reserve checked bytes once, no motion vector and no replay allocation/hash for report-only. Only verified genuine contact-gap schedule IDs may be skipped; no fabricated gap moves. Failure/cancellation drops unpublished bytes.

Reports distinguish software lifecycle/modal/wait/barrier/process/command-space semantics from excluded thermal/fan hardware truth, firmware/configuration/calibration, homing trajectory, collisions, support physics, mechanics/material and printability. Stationary recovery and priming are not deposited material. Successful replay is not permission to operate a printer.
