# Manufacturing boundary vision

Turn authoritative placed BREP, explicit process requirements and printer data into inspectable manufacturing artifacts with bounded geometric uncertainty, provenance and fresh independent replay. Isolate native planning and planar computation from persistence and protocol consumers.

One shared pipeline serves CLI and workbench: query-certified native material, source-preserving finishes, walls, skins, infill, deposited-anchor bridges and breakaway supports. Keep original material, mandatory reachable material and requested realization distinct. Geometry owns tolerance-aware source/material authority; the planner consumes it rather than recognizing fixtures or inventing successful fallbacks.

Durable value is a complete operator workflow—author, preflight, compile, inspect, save/reopen and export—not a preview or a schema declaration. Expand through explicit geometry, material, operation and resource contracts. Material painting, branching supports, new processes and execution require their own evidence gates. Software replay does not establish physical printability, machine readiness, collision safety or platform qualification.
