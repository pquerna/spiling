# Manufacturing boundary rules

Own native-section planar planning, inert specification realization and independent emitted-program verification. Depend inward on contracts and the native geometry facade; no storage, engine, filesystem import, TypeScript execution or alternate CAD kernel. Keep planner, backend and verifier distinct. Reuse immutable native definitions for occurrences.

`compile` accepts revision-captured definitions/occurrences/intent/ordered surface overlays, the immutable canonical image map and cancellation; successful bundles have undergone independent replay. Validate the exact image metadata/content correspondence including disabled layers. Share canonical image allocations into bundle provenance once; inspection replies contain metadata only. `verify_bundle` validates content, provenance, semantic fingerprint and bounded contracts before fresh replay; persisted verification flags are never authority. Manufacturing schema is 5, planner is 6, surface schema is 3; emitter/verifier remain 4 and SPLR stays 3. Old artifacts are refused, not migrated. Dimensions are right-handed Cartesian millimetres. Actual-height schedules retain nominal grid, exact top, horizontal, image-row and support-stop events with bounded local vertical-error refinement. Analytic eligibility remains conditional on every layer's topology, offsets and unchanged numerical/resource budgets. Bridges use actual prior deposited footprints; vertical breakaway supports remain clipped against all lower realized object/protected-hole clearance. No general STEP, alternative geometry or thin-feature repair.

Public `preflight` returns bounded revision-captured metadata eligibility checks with occurrence/face attribution where available. It checks explicit intent, native provenance, placement/envelope and orientation without claiming section/bridge/support analysis succeeded. Native analysis remains a Compile obligation and physical qualification remains absent.

Ordered definition/occurrence whole-face Smooth/VerticalRibs/CylindricalRibs/ImageRelief layers are the sole source-backed finish channel. Require exact native full-height rectangular supporting-face or full-turn exterior cylinder certification and the constrained upright bed-anchored extrusion lane. Share bounded scalar fields and robust material proofs, retaining carrier-specific native correspondence. Prove source/hole retention and bounded outward construction before ownership/caches/actual-anchor/support regeneration and clearance. Ribs retain strict first-wall peak survival; image resolution is a bounded attributed advisory over the actual admitted first wall, never a duplicate production construction or geometry fallback. Reuse native M separately from realized D: row/refinement events change D identities even inside invariant native slabs. Keep source area separate from realized and added area. Expand placement and conservative occurrence/component/prime exclusions without claiming physical collisions or grip. See [surface rules](src/planner/surface/AGENT-RULES.md) and [spatial image RFC](../../docs/adr/003-spatial-image-relief.md).

First-wall coverage distinguishes requested realized D from mandatory material. Assemble a borrowed required-material operand from original native M only on exactly attributed image-owned islands; every other island requires its full D. The same single admitted D erosion and conservative coverage dilation must reach that operand under the unchanged thin-region threshold/work bounds. Native-M thin/unreachable regions, source/hole retention and topology stay hard failures; unreachable fine image additions feed attributed advisory diagnostics rather than an error catch, second erosion or tolerance relaxation.


Keep native material sections, immutable shared wall layouts, disjoint skin/interior ownership, generated support ownership, path generation and pure process realization separate. Skin horizons are physical distances over actual scheduled layer heights. Sparse rectilinear fill preserves holes and reports nominal intentional void, not strength or physical coverage. Support-only layers have zero object ownership. Empty scheduled layers are only bounded breakaway contact gaps between bed-anchored support interface and actual object deposition; no leading/trailing empty layer or fake motion. Retain only admitted invariant material spans and one shared erosion layout per realized D span; charge all retained native/derived/wall/fill geometry, every layer, support and event against bounded budgets.

`inspect_bundle` borrows the immutable bundle and caller-owned exact asset hash, returning fresh report/captured provenance and optional `EncodedReplayDisplay {info,bytes}`. Report-only APIs allocate no replay buffers/index/hash. The independent decoder streams actual f64 XYZ/E/F directly to one checked-capacity SPLR byte sink only when selected; no copied motion vector, emitter helper or second parser. Corruption/cancellation drops unpublished bytes. Packed admission and bindings live in contracts; persistence/publication remains the engine's responsibility.

Artifacts remain SOFTWARE VALIDATION ONLY, NOT MACHINE READY. Explicit diagnostic-body and complete single-extruder Marlin workflows have distinct validated dialects; complete jobs declare machine/firmware/configuration/calibration identity, native Cartesian homing, integral Celsius waits, bounded deposition-excluded priming and final barriers/lift/park/shutdown. Decode actual emitted bytes independently, including first-layer/bridge speed-flow-cooling and retract/hop/recovery; separate object, support, prime and stationary recovery accounting. No printer connection, physical homing/thermal/fan truth, collisions, firmware/configuration qualification or printability certification. General/freeform STEP, branching supports, color and multi-axis/execution support are outside this envelope. Component shapes remain metadata; rendering establishes no GPU-visible/pickable or platform qualification.

Meaningful evidence exercises imported native corpus and repeated placed definitions through public compilation and independent replay, plus unsupported thin/support geometry, cancellation, envelope and resource rejection. Exercise `cargo test -p spiling-manufacturing` and the fresh-process durable CLI workflow; tests alone establish no physical support.
