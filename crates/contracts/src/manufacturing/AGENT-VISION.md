# Manufacturing contract vision

Provide one bounded inert Rust-defined language for printer/job data, explicit planar recipes, classified paths and independently replayed durable bundles. Core/compiler/native clients consume the same strict schema; protobuf adapters and generated shell DTOs do not establish another authority.

Schema4 captures the complete ordered source-face finish document and separates native source material from bounded realized outward additions. Whole-face vertical ribs are a native-derived manufacturing construction with a separate approximation bound, not BREP editing, a material change, generic procedural texture support or physical tactile certification.

One single-extruder complete Marlin2 job declares named machine/firmware/configuration/calibration provenance and typed native-home, thermal-wait, prime and final-park data. Diagnostic compilation remains explicit. Required process policy realizes first-layer/bridge speed/flow, queued cooling and external stationary retraction/travel-hop/recovery. True scheduled layer height and physical skins preserve variable-height object ownership; bounded bridge and explicit breakaway supports stay separate from object material. Genuine contact gaps have no fake deposition. These software semantics are not physical certification.

Opt-in SPLR schema3 carries actual object/support/process/lifecycle XYZ/E/F, source lines and validated role attribution through one independent parser/sink. Object/support reports, prime and recovered E remain separate; packed deposition includes all deposited roles. Homing trajectories remain firmware-owned and excluded.

Independent software decoding remains mandatory on compile and reopen. Command-space/modal/wait/barrier completeness does not certify firmware configuration truth, calibration, thermal sensor behavior, collision clearance or physical printability. Hardware selection, actual metadata/measurements and supervised physical prints remain unresolved external gates; no connector, arbitrary macros, multiple extruders, universal scheduler or physical qualification claim.
